Generate a 3D scene: "The Gates of Kordavos" — the opening encounter of a fantasy adventure.
A medieval frontier city gate during a harvest festival, seen from outside the walls, at the
moment travelers queue to be inspected by the city guard.

== SCENE BRIEF ==
Kordavos is a prosperous port capital on a wild frontier: native Valkaran culture (rustic,
carved-wood, nature motifs — trees, stags, knotwork) overlaid with the occupying Asterian
Empire's aesthetic (imperial, polished stone, gold trim, eagle/sun motifs). The gate itself is
ancient Valkaran stonework retrofitted with Asterian banners and hardware — that cultural
tension should read visually. Beyond the open gates, glimpses of a festival: stall canopies,
bunting, warm light, distant crowd. Outside the gates, a queue of travelers waits along the
road. Mood: welcoming but controlled — celebration behind a checkpoint.

== KEY ASSETS (priority order) ==
1. City gatehouse: twin towers flanking a large pointed stone archway, aged granite blocks,
   moss at the base, iron portcullis raised. Carved Valkaran knotwork frieze across the arch,
   partially worn. Height ~14 m.
2. Banners: two types hung together on the towers — (a) Asterian imperial: blue and gold,
   sun/eagle emblem, clean and new; (b) Valkaran festival: harvest-orange and green, tree/stag
   emblem, homespun cloth. Cloth should move naturally in a light wind.
3. Curtain walls extending left and right from the gatehouse, ~8 m tall, with wooden hoarding
   on top, fading toward the scene edges.
4. Queue set dressing: a dirt road leading to the gate with a waiting line of 8-12 background
   figures and props — a canvas-covered merchant cart with barrels, a mule, crates, sacks of
   grain, a pilgrim with a walking staff, a family with bundles.
5. Guard checkpoint at the arch: a wooden inspection table, a small brazier, a strongbox for
   the 3-mark entry toll, two spears leaning on the wall.
6. Hero character: Garlan Ironfist, a burly human guard — stern face, kind eyes, well-groomed
   beard, polished half-plate armor, blue-and-gold tabard with the city crest, one hand raised
   beckoning the next traveler forward.
7. Festival glimpse through the arch: market stall canopies (striped canvas), strings of
   pennant bunting, hanging lanterns, a sense of dense, lively crowd as a backdrop layer.

== SPATIAL LAYOUT ==
Road runs along the Z axis toward the gate at origin. Queue snakes along the road from
Z = +25 m to the checkpoint at Z = +3 m. Gatehouse spans X = -8 to +8 m. Festival backdrop
layer behind the arch at Z = -10 to -30 m, framed by the archway opening. Low rolling
grassland with scattered wildflowers and a few birch trees on either side of the road.

== LIGHTING / ATMOSPHERE ==
Late-morning autumn sun, warm golden key light from high camera-left, soft blue sky with a few
cumulus clouds. Light haze/dust in the air; god rays through the arch from the brighter
festival side. Autumn palette: golden grass, amber and rust foliage, warm stone. The space
through the archway should glow slightly warmer and brighter than the foreground — the
festival is the promise, the gate is the obstacle.

== CAMERA (for preview renders) ==
