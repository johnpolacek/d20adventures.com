Wiki Adventure Implementation Merge Review

Deep review of the merged feature/wiki-adventure-implementation worktree after it landed on main. The review covers runtime flow, Convex guards, admin authoring, S3 source behavior, migrated Myr content, validation, and durable wiki updates.

Bottom line
Focused wiki-adventure tests, TypeScript, and production build pass. Repository-wide Biome check fails and several rollout risks remain.

What landed

Four Realm of Myr adventures now have repo-local wiki source and bridge checks: The Midnight Summons, Covert Cargo, The Road to Kordavos, and March of Davos. Runtime create/start/advance paths can load compiled wiki artifacts for registered local wiki adventures.

Strong points

Turn advancement now assembles a bounded wiki context packet, validates LLM transition targets, pins content hashes, and commits through a Convex mutation that rejects stale turn, stale encounter, stale content, and duplicate order writes.

Not a full cutover

Legacy S3 AdventurePlan JSON remains in the public selection and startup path for listing, titles, party size, and route compatibility. Wiki source drives registered runtime encounters, but old content architecture has not been removed.
