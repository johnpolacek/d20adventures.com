Manual Test Script

  1. Open /settings/realm-of-myr and choose The Midnight Summons.
  2. Confirm the character selection screen shows Thalbern and preserves the existing Details/Select behavior.
  3. Select Thalbern and confirm the solo adventure auto-starts rather than stopping in a multiplayer lobby.
  4. Confirm the created Adventure pins wiki content, creates/copies Thalbern's runtime character sheet, creates the first turn, and starts at broken-silence.
  5. Submit a cautious investigation action and confirm the AI GM requests or resolves an appropriate perception/stealth path.
  6. Force or choose a failed/unsafe path and confirm transition to owlbear-confrontation.
  7. Exercise a critical-health or rescue path and confirm timely-rescue or Wollandora intervention behaves consistently.
  8. Reach meeting-at-the-stones and choose whether to help Wollandora, go home, or learn about missing relics.
  9. Complete one accepted-mission path into preparing-for-the-city and one refusal path into back-home; both should behave as terminal encounters.
  10. Confirm the final turn sets isFinalEncounter, the Adventure becomes completed, endedAt is set, and the completion UI appears.
  11. Confirm the evolved Adventure wiki summary, discoveries, entity updates, open threads, resolved threads, final outcome, and transition metadata match the playthrough.
  12. Click Play Again and verify a new Adventure instance starts without mutating the completed playthrough record.

Acceptance Gates

  • Public listing and character selection remain visually and behaviorally compatible with the screenshots.
  • The full legacy adventure root, section/scene framing, all 7 encounters, all 11 transition edges, both NPCs, Thalbern, and all image refs are represented in wiki source or documented as intentionally transformed.
  • Adventure creation pins the compiled content version/hash or preview draft ID.
  • Turn advancement reads compiled wiki artifacts, not legacy nested AdventurePlan sections/scenes.
  • Illegal transitions are rejected before Convex writes.
  • Thalbern remains player-controlled; AI does not mutate his sheet without validated runtime patch semantics.
  • wollandora-intervention is corrected, aliased, or blocked as an invalid legacy transition before publish.
  • Terminal encounters with no outgoing transitions complete the Adventure and render the final completion controls.
  • Play Again starts a separate Adventure instance pinned to content and leaves the completed record immutable except for allowed reporting/admin annotations.
  • Reports/admin views can read the evolved Adventure playthrough record.
