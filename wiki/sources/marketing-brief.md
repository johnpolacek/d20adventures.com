# Marketing brief

[Home](../index.md) · [Sources](../Sources.md) · [Roadmap](../roadmap.md)

Reviewed against repository surfaces on 2026-10-01. Live campaigns and conversion metrics were not reviewed.

The product language centers on an AI Game Master, narrative RPG adventures, D20 rolls, authored modules, and playing through a story with friends.

Public surfaces include the homepage, about/contact pages, mailing list, roadmap, and legal pages. The homepage now serves a static public shell and loads the signed-in player's active adventure separately.

Community creation and the stage-first play experience are roadmap items. Marketing should distinguish them from the current playable text, narration, and 2D map features.

Launch status, audience size, conversion performance, and the preferred primary call to action remain unconfirmed.
