# Textures

Most textures here were painted by `scripts/stage-textures.ts`. These came from Poly Haven instead, CC0, so they need no credit. They are listed so their source is known.

| File | Poly Haven asset | What was done to it |
|---|---|---|
| `hull-planks.jpg` | `weathered_planks`, 2K diffuse | The seams between its planks were cut out and the boards joined, since the `painted` material lays its own seams. Brightened, then saved at 1536 px tall. The barge hull at the Mordava pier. |
| `bark-willow.jpg` | `bark_willow`, 2K diffuse | Saved at 1536. The bark of the Mordava pier's trees. |
| `bark-willow-normal.jpg` | `bark_willow`, 2K normal (OpenGL) | Saved at 1536, the `normal` of the same `image` material. |
