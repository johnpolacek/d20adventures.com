Another screen capture of [D20 Adventures](https://d20adventures.com), a game where you create an adventure plan with detailed notes and assets, to be run by an AI GM using 5E rules.

This adventure is solo with a premade character. Other adventures will be multiplayer and let you make your own characters.

It’s in alpha. I have plenty of fixes and improvements to make (as you’ll see if you watch the clip).

Changes since the [last gameplay clip](/updates/gameplay-11-19-2026):

**Stage and Visuals**

- The turn marker is a thin indigo rim with slowly turning square dots.
- Thalbern and the Owlbear are rigged [Hi3D](/updates/turn-your-character-art-into-animated-3d-figures-with-hi3d-and-blender) models.
- Thalbern’s hands grip properly, and his elbows and forearms bend right.
- Valkarr woods trees have roots, moss, finer leaves, dead limbs and saplings.
- Mist fades out near the camera, so orbiting shots no longer show it as a wall.

**Sound**

- New sound engine with combat cues and ambience.
- Characters make body sounds: footsteps in time with walking, falls, ducking into cover, beast growls.
- Footsteps match the floor, and huge creatures land heavily.

**GM and Rules**

- Questions stay in the scene. NPCs answer, no filler while a question waits.
- Heroes aren’t offered spells when there’s no target. A typed spell with no target asks first.

**Play UI**

- Narration uses the Literata font, with drop caps and scene ornaments.
- The prompt card has a shorter question in gold and no hero name label.
- Suggestion pills load at full size and fade in.
- UI controls in both the site and the app now come from UI SDK, with Radix removed.

You can play the solo adventure online, or better yet, download the app and run it on your own machine with Claude, then invite people to play with you. It’s all at [d20adventures.com](https://d20adventures.com).
