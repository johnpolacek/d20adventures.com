This one played itself. The [D20 Adventures](https://d20adventures.com) desktop app now has an autopilot that plays an adventure from a script and records the screen with its sound, so I can capture a run without touching anything. Sorry for the sound quality, that will be fixed next time. Thalbern hides from the Owlbear, it gives up and wanders off, and he meets Wollandora at the stones.

Changes since the [last gameplay clip](/updates/gameplay-11-21-2026):

**Stage and Camera**

- The camera directs each turn, picking its shot from what the moment is. A tense line pushes in close, a quiet exchange goes over the shoulder.
- It only frames the cast from where nothing hides them, so no more crits seen from behind a tree.
- Close-ups on the Owlbear show its head and shoulders, not the top of its head.
- The Owlbear walks back into the trees when its search comes up empty.
- Scene changes hold the last picture until the next scene is built and framed.

**Narration**

- A narrator reads the story aloud in the desktop app, with [Kokoro](https://github.com/hexgrad/kokoro) running on your own machine.
- Dice rolls play in full once per encounter, then quicker, and a tap skips to the result.

**Desktop App**

- It runs on Intel Macs, Windows and Linux now.
- Adventures download their art and 3D figures as you play.

**Site**

- Each adventure has its own page, with Play and Host buttons.
- Panels, lists and screens animate in and out across the site and the app.

Still plenty to fix. Give it a try at [d20adventures.com](https://d20adventures.com).
