A new adventure this time. [Covert Cargo](https://d20adventures.com/settings/realm-of-myr/covert-cargo) is multiplayer, with three premade heroes instead of one. Lyra is a scholar hired to check a smuggled Valkaran relic on a riverboat, while Poppen and Thalbern each watch the deal from the bank, not knowing about each other.

Changes since the [last gameplay clip](/updates/gameplay-11-22-2026):

**Stage and Camera**

- The stage keeps time with the narrator. The camera moves and people enter as their paragraph is read.
- Characters sneak, walk, run and sprint at the pace the story gives them.
- A hero who readies a weapon holds it, like Thalbern’s bow through his walk and his shot.
- A critical hit throws its target back, and a fumble leaves the attacker stumbling.
- A blow that lands has weight, with a brief freeze as it strikes.
- The screen’s edges redden when a hero is badly hurt.
- A hidden hero stays in his hiding spot until the story brings him out.
- The fallen lie on the floor, and a dying hero falls on the paragraph that hurts him.
- Shots in the cramped cabin skip angles where someone blocks the view.
- Mist drifts.

**Covert Cargo**

- The riverboat is a timber river barge now, with open shutters you can see and shoot through.
- Poppen and Reinhard have new figures.
- When Silas hands over the scrollcase, you see it cross to Aelar.

**Sound**

- Story beats can play sounds: doors, a splash, a rope cut, a bowstring.
- The pier at night has river, crickets and wind, and the saloon has creaking timbers.
- The ambience follows the camera and dips under the narrator.
- Busy moments no longer pile up into a smear of sound.

**Narration**

- Men’s lines are read in a man’s voice.
- The narrator says the realm’s names right, like Silas and Valkaran.
- A Pause button stops the narrator mid-sentence.

**GM and Rules**

- The GM knows who can’t see whom, and doesn’t narrate a view between them.
- Characters can climb over a rail, a sill or a crate.
- Characters walk around foes in the way, and shove them aside in a doorway.
- Water is swimmable, and moves stop at the bank unless they mean to go in.
- No opportunity attacks when there’s no fight on.

**Site**

- The About page tells the story of D20 Adventures, from the text version to the 3D gates.
- The [Vision](/vision) page tracks the road to Baldur’s Gate 3 as a free, open source platform.

Covert Cargo runs in the desktop app, where you can host it and invite friends to play the other heroes. It’s all at [d20adventures.com](https://d20adventures.com).
