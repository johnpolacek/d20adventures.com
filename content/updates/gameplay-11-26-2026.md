The first time the game opens, it welcomes you and shows you around the screen before the story starts. Thalbern hides from the Owlbear on the trail at midnight, then meets Wollandora at the Standing Stones.

Changes since the [last gameplay clip](/updates/gameplay-11-24-2026):

**First Look**

- A tour lights each part of the screen in turn: the story, who acts, the camera, the map, the journal and Scene Settings.
- The party screen has Play with Tour beside Play.
- Nothing in the scene happens until the tour is done, then the narrator reads the opening.

**Stage and Camera**

- An encounter opens on a wide establishing shot that slowly circles the place.
- The Owlbear prowls out of the dark at a slow, heavy pace, and the camera lets it come.
- Every new story card moves the camera.
- A crouched hero is filmed at his crouched height, not over the top of his head.
- Depth of field blurs what lies behind whoever the camera looks at, on high and ultra.
- Far grass and ferns no longer flicker.
- Things handed over pass from hand to hand, and stay held.
- Ray spells read as rays, cast from the hand.
- Past rounds can be read again, with everyone placed where that round left them.

**GM and Rules**

- Heroes level up at milestones, like Baldur’s Gate 3, and never mid-fight.
- The four Realm of Myr adventures hand off to each other. Wollandora sends Thalbern to the smugglers’ pier from [Covert Cargo](https://d20adventures.com/settings/realm-of-myr/covert-cargo).
- Scene intros say less.
- Each GM call runs in its own conversation, which halves the tokens sent to Claude.

**Site**

- The web demo can read its story aloud, with [Kokoro](https://github.com/hexgrad/kokoro) running in your browser, and plays its scene’s sounds.
- Updates, About and Contact have new title pages with pen and ink engravings.

Play the solo adventure online, or download the app, run it with Claude on your own machine and invite friends to play. It’s all at [d20adventures.com](https://d20adventures.com).
