Thalbern walks into [The Midnight Summons](https://d20adventures.com) now. Not a painted cutout. A rigged 3D figure that strolls in, draws his bow and swings a sword.

Getting there took most of the week. I made figures with Rodin, Meshy and Hi3D, played animation clips from Quaternius and Mesh2Motion on them, and scored the results side by side. Hi3D won. Thalbern, Wollandora and the owlbear are all Hi3D figures in the game now.

Hands were the hard part. A hand has to close around a grip, hang at ease, and stay a fist when it's a fist. Elbows have to stay elbows.

Other things that landed:

- Sound. Combat cues and night ambience in the Valkarr woods.
- Hiding. Slip into thick growth and watch the owlbear come out. Enemies can still find you with Passive Perception or a Search.
- A ring on the ground marks whose turn it is.
- Speech is painted into the scene instead of sitting in bubbles.
- Narration is set in Literata, with drop caps.

225 commits this week. The whole thing is open source on [Github](https://github.com/johnpolacek/d20adventures.com).
