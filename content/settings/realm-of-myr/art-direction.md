Authoritative look reference for every 3D scene set in this setting. Materials, palette and heraldry here override anything a model infers from encounter text. Not part of the wiki graph: the scene pipeline reads this file directly (scripts/scene-pipeline/brief.ts).

## Overall

Stylized-realistic fantasy: readable painterly forms with physically based materials and believable wear. Not photoscan, not cartoon. No lettering on any surface; emblems and heraldry only. Two cultures share every Kordavos scene, and the tension between them should read visually: old Valkaran bones with Asterian dressing bolted on.

## Valkaran (native, traditional)

- Materials: rough-dressed granite and fieldstone with moss at the base, oak and dark timber, carved wood, homespun and undyed cloth, iron rather than brass, antler and bone.
- Motifs: trees (oaks), stags, interlaced knotwork friezes, harvest sheaves. Carving is deep and partially worn.
- Palette: harvest orange, forest and moss green, oak brown, ochre, undyed cream. Cloth is faded and sun-worn.
- Emblem: a dark oak tree flanked by a frontal stag's head with antlers, on harvest orange with a green base band and a thin gold rule.
- Sacred and ancient Valkaran spaces (Church of Valkara, the Old Grand Library, the lost temple of Old Valkara): arched ceilings, stained glass of Valkaran saints, ivy on stone; the oldest work is translucent white marble with a faint inner glow, pillars carved as ancient oaks, vaulted ceilings painted with constellations, silver-etched runes.

## Asterian (occupying empire)

- Materials: polished ashlar stone, marble, gold trim and gilt hardware, burnished steel plate, fine dyed silk and wool, wrought iron with gilt finials.
- Motifs: sun rays, the eagle with spread wings, laurel, clean geometric borders. Everything new, crisp and symmetrical.
- Palette: imperial navy blue and gold, white marble, polished steel, crimson accents.
- Emblem: a gold sunburst with a gold eagle at its centre on navy blue.
- City of Kordavos crest (worn by the city guard, hung on civic buildings): a gold tower beneath a small gold sun on a navy shield with a gold border.
- City guard: polished half-plate with gold-trimmed edges, navy tabard bearing the city crest, steel kettle helm or open helm, spear and round shield in navy and gold.

## Kordavos, the city

- A prosperous port capital on a wild frontier. Ancient Valkaran stonework retrofitted with Asterian banners, lamps and iron.
- Outer walls and gatehouse: aged granite blocks, moss and grime at the base, timber hoarding along the wall tops, iron portcullis, twin towers with conical timber roofs, both cultures' banners hung together.
- Streets and market: cobbles and flagstones, timber-framed houses with jettied upper floors, striped canvas stall awnings, pennant bunting, hanging lanterns. Harvest Festival dressing is Valkaran: sheaves, gourds, orange and green cloth.
- Asterian Quarter and the Blackthorn Mansion: pale ashlar, towering spires, wrought-iron gates bearing both cultures' symbols, cobblestone carriage courtyard, grand exterior staircase, torch-lit facades. Interiors gilt, marble, chandeliers, masks and finery.
- Docks: salt-weathered timber piers on pilings, tall ships at berth, crates, barrels, rope, gulls, warm low sun on water. Leather aprons and sweat, not finery.
- Lower District: narrow leaning tenements that block the sun, dilapidated timber and stained plaster, dim alleys, doors that close quickly. Damp, stale, shadowed.
- Taverns: the Dragonbone Inn is warm oak, a large stone hearth, roasting meat and a bard, mixed clientele. The Silver Serpent is a sailors' den: sticky floors, a sea chart carved into one wall, nautical relics behind the bar, dim and salt-stained.
- Beneath the city: hand-carved tunnels and catacombs, cool damp stone, iron smell, torch-lit, support pillars, barrels. Deeper still is Old Valkara (see Valkaran sacred spaces).

## Factions, as they dress

- Blackthorn Clan: Asterian-aligned wealth. Gilt, black and gold, masks, tailored uniforms on servants.
- Raskov Clan: smugglers. Dock and black-market dressing, oilskin, rope, unmarked crates, lantern light.
- Valtor Clan and the Church of Valkara: traditionalist Valkaran. Robes in green and undyed wool, oak and antler, stained glass.
- Covenant of the Cowl: hooded figures in dark undyed cloaks, faces hidden, no visible heraldry.

## Light and atmosphere defaults

- Exteriors by day: warm golden autumn sun, soft blue sky with cumulus, light haze and dust, autumn palette in grass and foliage.
- Interiors: lit by their own hearths, candles and lanterns; warm pools in cool shadow.
- Underground: single-source torchlight, long shadows, damp sheen on stone.
- Night exteriors: cool blue ambient with warm torch and window light.
