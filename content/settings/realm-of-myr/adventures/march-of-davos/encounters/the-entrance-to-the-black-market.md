## Intro

Between two crumbling buildings stands a makeshift entrance, a few old planks nailed together, bare but for a faint snake-and-moon scratched into the beam above. To those who know what to look for, it is unmistakably the hidden doorway to the Black Market. A heavy cloth curtain hangs over it, swaying in the faint breeze and hiding what lies beyond.

{actor: garrick} A burly figure in a tattered cloak stands guard, his face mostly hidden beneath a hood. His arms are crossed, and a long dagger is tucked in plain view in his belt. He eyes the adventurers with suspicion as they approach.

{speaker: garrick, delivery: a low and gravelly grunt} Looking for something?

{actor: garrick} He steps forward, blocking the entrance with his broad frame, and waits to see if the adventurers are worthy of entry.

## GM Notes

"No one gets in without the passphrase." 

The adventurers have several strategies at their disposal to gain access to the Black Market. They might rely on diplomacy or charm, using Charisma-based skills like Persuasion or Deception to sweet-talk or bluff their way past the guard. If they've overheard or gathered the passphrase through investigation or by asking the right people, they could simply recall it and gain entry. Alternatively, bribery is a practical option, with a well-timed offer of gold convincing the guard to look the other way. If the group prefers more aggressive tactics, they could use intimidation or threats to force the guard to let them through, showing their combat prowess or making veiled threats.

For a stealthier approach, they could attempt to sneak past using Dexterity (Stealth) or employ magic like Invisibility to bypass the guard unnoticed. If they find themselves without the necessary information, they might return to the streets to gather intelligence from the locals, using persuasion or intimidation to learn the passphrase or gain insights into how to bypass the guard. Magic spells like Charm Person, Suggestion, or even Disguise Self can also help manipulate the situation, giving the adventurers a creative edge in gaining access to the Black Market.

If they simply attack, the guard will call for reinforcements, a group of six henchmen. The henchmen beat the party down with clubs and do not kill. Downed heroes come to in the alley outside, bruised, with the curtain still guarded.

**Fixed facts.** Today's passphrase is "The moon keeps no ledger." Garrick lets in anyone who gives it. A bribe of 10 gold or more, or a Persuasion, Deception or Intimidation check against DC 14, also works. A failed check costs a round of haggling or a second try with another approach, not the market.

**No one forces the door.** A hero who shoves past Garrick finds two henchmen stepping out through the curtain to block it, and Garrick whistles them back only for the passphrase, a bribe or a passed check. Garrick never waves the party in for nothing, not even to end a fight. A brawl ends when the party stands down and pays, or is beaten down. Garrick knows nothing of Joran and says nothing of Silverhand. He only keeps the door.

Move on once Garrick lets the party through or they slip past him. If they have not got in by the end of the third round, he takes a bribe of whatever they offer and waves them in.
