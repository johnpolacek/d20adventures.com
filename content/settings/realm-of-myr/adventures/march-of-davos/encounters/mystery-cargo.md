## Intro

Following Loric’s directions through a maze of warehouses and narrow alleys, the adventurers find Warehouse 14, a storage shed tucked in a shadowed corner. The air is stagnant here, away from the sea breeze.

{actor: Alaric Fleyven} Suspicious figures lurk by the entrance, posing as workers but clearly on alert. One, a tall man with a jagged scar down his cheek, spots the adventurers and signals. His companions draw short swords from beneath their tunics.

{speaker: Alaric Fleyven, delivery: demanding} Who’re you lot? Ain’t nothing here for you. Best be on your way before you find trouble you can’t walk away from.

## GM Notes

This encounter is a combat-focused scene that transitions into a discovery phase. Alaric and his 3 thugs will fight if the players don't leave immediately.

### The Warehouse Skirmish

- **Alaric** is a capable rogue. He uses the crates for cover and attempts to flank players.
- **The Thugs** are simple mercenaries hired by the Blackthorn Clan to guard this specific shipment.
- **The Environment:** The warehouse is filled with crates. These can be used as difficult terrain or cover. 

If Alaric is reduced to 5 HP or less, he may try to flee or surrender in exchange for his life. He reveals he was told to hold the cargo until "the Lady" came for it, and was paid in Blackthorn silver. He was never told her name and does not know it, but some other 'cowl-wearing freaks' have been sniffing around. Asked what they look like, he says: dark undyed cloaks with deep hoods, faces never shown, no badge or colours, and they move in twos at night and speak little. He adds that Silas, one of the Lady's own men, brought the big crate in by river and said nobody was to open it. If the story so far says the crate never came, he says instead that the Lady is in a fury over a crate lost upriver. A surrendered Alaric is the heroes' prisoner. Alaric never takes up his sword again once he surrenders. He lets it fall, reaches for nothing, and does as the heroes say for the rest of the scene. Taken outside, he slips away in the panic over the drake and is gone.

### The Discovery

Once the thugs are dealt with, the players can search the warehouse. Among crates of elven wine and silk, they find a small, reinforced lockbox with its key left in the lock. Inside is a **Manifest and Map**. Any hero who searches the shed finds the lockbox, and it opens with that key, no roll. The chained crate's padlock opens to thieves' tools at DC 14, or Athletics DC 16 breaks the chain.

- **The Manifest:** Lists several artifacts from Old Valkara, including the "Key of Ilmarin" and the Oath Stone of the First Clans. However, the Key is missing from the crate.
- **The Chained Crate:** Against the back wall stands an iron-banded oak crate hung with chain, far too heavy to shift. Inside is a great seat of carved stone. The manifest names it the Oath Stone of the First Clans, "brought by river, proven by an arcanist." If the story so far says the crate never reached Kordavos, it is not here, and the manifest lists the Oath Stone as due by river and not delivered.
- **The Oath Stone's aura:** Detect Magic shows a strong, old enchantment on the seat and nothing else in the warehouse. An Arcana check of DC 18, or 18 or higher on the spell's check, tells its purpose: binding promises, loyalty and solemn vows. It does nothing when touched or sat in. Detect Evil and Good finds it neither hallowed nor desecrated.
- **The Map:** Shows a route through the Valkarr Forest with a mark on a ship currently docked at the far end of the harbor: *The Star of Faelendar*.

**Reaching the lockbox mid-fight.** A hero who pushes past the blades to the lockbox while they still fight does so on Athletics or Acrobatics DC 15.

**Bluffs and bribes.** A bluff, such as posing as a delivery, is Deception DC 15. It buys one round of talk and no look inside. Alaric takes no bribe. He is paid in Blackthorn silver and fears the Lady more.

**Intimidation.** A hero who beats DC 15 Intimidation, or shows Alaric he is outmatched, makes the three blades lower their swords. Persuasion DC 15, offering their lives and a way out, does the same. Alaric then surrenders as if at 5 HP. Once two of his blades are down, he is outmatched, and he surrenders on his next turn. No watch comes to a whistle or shout.

**The Trigger:** As the players finish their search, a massive, bone-shaking crash echoes from the docks outside, followed by a scream that pierces the air. If the fight is still on at the end of its third round, the crash comes then instead. From the crash on, no one attacks or holds the door. The thugs drop their swords and run out at once, each for himself, Alaric surrenders, and the party has one round to search before the screams draw them out.

**Pace.** Close the scene at the end of the round after the crash, with every hero heading out toward the screams together. A hero still inside comes running. If no hero has opened the lockbox by then, a hero snatches it up on the way out, still shut. Alaric and any downed thug stay behind.
