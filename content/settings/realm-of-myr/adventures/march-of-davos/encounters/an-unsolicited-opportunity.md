## Intro

As late evening falls upon the Dragonbone Inn, the air thickens with the scent of roasted meats and the sound of mugs clinking. Amidst the revelry, several locals eye the adventurers with curious intent. 

Among them is Beric, a burly dockworker with a scar across his face, and Eamon, a scholarly type with an aura of intelligence. Beric speaks first, his voice gruff but respectful, "Ye seem like quite an assorted lot. We could use some folks of varied talents like you lot."

Eamon, adjusting his spectacles, adds, "Indeed, we have matters that require the expertise of seasoned adventurers. Intricacies that only your kind might handle. Perhaps we could discuss this privately?"

## The Meeting

Beric and Eamon lead the adventurers to a dimly lit backroom. A sturdy table occupies the center of the room, with mismatched chairs pulled haphazardly around it. 

"We've got a bit of a situation," Beric begins, his voice low. "A shipment went missin' from the docks. Valuable cargo, meant for a noble up in the Asterian quarter. The people I work for ain't too pleased."

Eamon leans forward. "Among the cargo were certain... artifacts. Items that could cause quite a stir if they fall into the wrong hands. We believe a group of smugglers operating out of the lower district may be responsible. However, our hands are tied—discretion is paramount. We need you to recover it. Quietly."

## GM Notes

- **Beric** is a dockworker who knows the rough side of Kordavos. He speaks bluntly.
- **Eamon** is a half-elf scholar with hidden ties to the Blackthorn family (though he won't reveal this). He is more refined and calculating.
- **The Deal**: They offer gold for the recovery of the shipment. They mention the "Silver Serpent Tavern" at the docks as a place where rumors of the theft might be circulating.
- **Secret**: Beric and Eamon are actually affiliated with the Raskov Clan, but they want to use an outside group that can't be traced back to them.

**Note**: This encounter concludes the first day. After the negotiation, the party should take a Long Rest at the inn.
