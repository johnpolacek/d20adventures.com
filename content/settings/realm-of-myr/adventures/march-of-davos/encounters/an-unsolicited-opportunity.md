## Intro

Late evening falls on the Dragonbone Inn amid the clink of mugs. Several locals eye the adventurers with interest, among them Beric, a burly dockworker with a scar across his face, and Eamon, a sharp-minded scholar.

{speaker: Beric Sonacus, delivery: gruff but respectful} Ye seem quite an assorted lot. We could use folks of varied talents like you.

{actor: Eamon Vellis} Eamon adjusts his spectacles.

{speaker: Eamon Vellis} Indeed, we have matters that require the expertise of seasoned adventurers. Perhaps we could discuss this privately?

## GM Notes

**The meeting.** Once the party agrees to talk, Beric and Eamon lead them to a dim backroom with a sturdy table and mismatched chairs. There the two make their offer in these lines, each said once.

Beric, low: "We've got a situation. A crate came down the Mordava on a river tug yesterday, at first light. It never reached the warehouse it was bound for. The people I work for ain't pleased."

Eamon: "It holds old pieces, the kind that cause a stir in the wrong hands. Find where it went and tell us. Quietly. And do not open it."

**What they know.** These facts do not change. Beyond them, Beric and Eamon say they do not know.
- One crate, iron-banded oak hung with chain, the size of a cart bed. It takes four strong men or a cart to move.
- It came down the Mordava on a river tug at first light yesterday and was unloaded at the river pier.
- It was bound for a noble's warehouse on the quay, and never arrived. The pier hands say men with the right papers carted it off.
- They do not know who moved it or where it is now.
- Asked who sent it or whose warehouse it was bound for, they say they were not told. Asked what is inside, Eamon says "old pieces" and no more. They never say they know a thing and will not tell it.
- The Silver Serpent Tavern by the docks is where dockhands drink and talk. Start there.

**The reward.** Ten gold to the company now as earnest if they accept. Two hundred gold to the company when they bring word of where the crate is. They need not move it. Once, for a Persuasion check of DC 15, Eamon raises it to three hundred.

**Who they are.**
- **Beric Sonacus** works the river pier and knows the rough side of Kordavos. He speaks bluntly.
- **Eamon Vellis** is a half-elf scholar, refined and calculating.
- **Secret:** Both work for the Blackthorns, and neither will say so. The Blackthorns hired Eamon to prove the crate's contents genuine before Lord Thaddeus paid for them. Eamon knows the crate holds a carved stone seat of Old Valkara, and keeps it to himself. No roll makes him say it. A Persuasion or Insight check of DC 15 gets only "old Valkaran pieces, and valuable." Someone inside the house diverted the crate, and they hire outsiders because they do not know whom in the house to trust. Asked outright, they deny any tie to the Blackthorns. Insight DC 15 shows Beric means a noble house when he says "the people I work for."
- **If the story so far says** the tug never left the pier or lost its crate there, the crate never reached Kordavos. Beric says the tug never came in, and they want to know what became of it upriver. The reward is the same.

**Bram's prices** from the common room still stand: a bed 2 silver a head with supper and breakfast, ale 2 copper. He does not haggle, and slides back short coin with the price said again.

**Ending the night.** This scene closes the first day. Any hero may answer for the company, and AI heroes may accept or refuse without waiting on the others.
- Once the heroes accept or refuse, close the scene at that round's end. Beric and Eamon leave, with the earnest left on the table if the heroes accepted. Bram calls the last bell, and the heroes go up to their beds and sleep through the night. They need not say they rest.
- If they have not answered by the end of the third round, Eamon leaves the earnest on the table and says the offer stands until they find the crate. Then close the scene the same way.

## Transitions

- To [[encounter:morning-at-the-dragonbone]] when the heroes have accepted or refused the job, or the third round has ended. The heroes sleep the night at the inn.
