## Intro

{actor: astrid-blackthorn} Astrid wipes the black ichor of the undead from her blade, breathing hard, and looks to the far archway with awe and dread.

{speaker: astrid-blackthorn, delivery: breathless} The catacombs were a decoy. This is the heart of Old Valkara.

Through the archway, rough stone gives way to impossible architecture. Walls of translucent white marble glow with a faint inner starlight. Towering pillars carved as ancient oaks hold up a vaulted ceiling painted with the constellations of a sky Kordavos hasn't seen in a thousand years.

At the center of this hidden cathedral stands a grand altar, empty, the air around it rippling with heat. Faded runes in silver filigree tell the story of the "Key of Ilmarin," a gift from the stars to the first High King of Valkara.

The silence is heavy with centuries. Then, from a dark alcove in the wall behind the altar, a soft, familiar chuckle breaks it.

## GM Notes

This room serves as the threshold to the final encounter. The ripples of heat and the silver runes indicate the powerful artifacts that were once kept here. The laughter belongs to Joran, who has already reached the sanctum. He stands in a narrow alcove in the wall behind the altar, where the starlight does not reach. The alcove is never found empty. As soon as anyone approaches the altar, calls to him or looks behind it, the scene ends and Final Confrontation begins with Joran stepping out. Do not play Joran in this scene: no NPC turn shows him or speaks for him.

## Transitions

- To [[encounter:final-confrontation]] when anyone approaches the altar, calls into the dark, or searches for the source of the laughter. Joran steps out of the alcove behind the altar.
