## Intro

{actor: astrid-blackthorn} The ballroom’s murmur quiets as Lady Astrid Blackthorn steps forward to address the crowd. She smiles proudly, and her eyes linger on the adventurers for a moment as she scans the room.

{speaker: astrid-blackthorn, delivery: smooth and commanding} Ladies and gentlemen, honored guests, tonight we celebrate not just our prosperity but our alliance with the ancient people of the Valkarr Forest. I am pleased to introduce a very special guest, an emissary from our elven allies. Please welcome Aelindor, master bard of Faelendar, who will honor us with the Song of the Eldertree.

{actor: aelindor} The grand doors swing open, and all eyes turn as a tall, ethereal elf strides in. His silver hair shimmers in the chandelier light, and his long robes are green and silver. On his back is an ornately carved lyre of ancient heartwood, its strings glinting like threads of moonlight.

{actor: aelindor, target: astrid-blackthorn} Aelindor steps to the center of the ballroom, nods respectfully to Astrid, and faces the crowd.

{speaker: aelindor, delivery: smooth as flowing water} It is an honor to stand before you. Tonight I share a song of my people, the Song of the Eldertree. It speaks of the forest’s eternal wisdom and the bond between our people and this land.

{actor: aelindor} He strums the lyre, and a soft, haunting melody fills the air, as if the forest itself whispers through the strings. The music draws the crowd in, and for a moment the ballroom seems to stand in the heart of the ancient woods.

It would also make the perfect distraction for anyone wishing to slip away.
