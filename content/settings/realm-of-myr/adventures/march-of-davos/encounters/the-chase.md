## Intro

The ballroom is in chaos. Anyone who peers over the balcony feels the cool night air and glimpses the hooded figure sliding down the rope into the dark garden below.

{actor: covenant-assassin} Among the lush greenery and manicured flower beds, the figure disappears through what looks like an old drainage grate hidden beneath the ivy. The grate, almost indistinguishable from the garden's stone paths, swings shut with a soft clang, its mechanism hidden beneath the vines.

## GM Notes

The assassin is through the grate before any hero reaches it. After the intro he is never seen in this scene, not at the grate, not in the garden, not below. He is not caught.

Legacy stages:

### The Sealed Grate

Anyone who reaches the grate finds it sealed impossibly tight. Runes etched into its iron pulse softly beneath the ivy, and a faint hum of magic lingers in the air.

No matter how hard they push, pull, or pry, the grate stays shut, locked by powerful magic. The runes are Valkaran warding script, a mortal ward, no god's work and no Church seal. They read "Shut to all but the Cowl." Picks find no latch. The assassin has fled beneath it, and without the means to dispel the seal, the adventurers are thwarted for now.

In the aftermath of the assassination, the once-celebratory atmosphere in the Blackthorn mansion turns to utter panic. Thaddeus Blackthorn lies lifeless, his body crumpled on the ballroom floor, the dark poison having already claimed his life. Guests scream and rush toward the exits, while guards frantically search for the assassin, who has long since disappeared. 

The house is shut. Guards hold every stair and door, and no hero gets back into the mansion's rooms tonight. Heroes who stayed in the ballroom are sent out to the garden by the guards to look for the killer. Once the heroes are down, the guards haul up the rope and send back down any hero who climbs it. The guards ask and urge, but do not blame, threaten or arrest the party.

**Pace.** At the end of the second round, Lady Astrid comes down into the garden with lanterns and house guards, changed into her ranger's leathers, and the hunt begins.
