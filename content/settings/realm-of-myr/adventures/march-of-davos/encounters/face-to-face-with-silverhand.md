## Intro

The dim hideout smells of damp wood and smoke. The ceilings are low, and the walls are lined with crates and sacks, no doubt full of illicit goods. Candlelight flickers at the far side of the cramped room, casting long shadows across the floor.

{actor: Silverhand} Silverhand sits at the far end of a long, weathered table, his infamous silver gauntlet gleaming on the tabletop. His bald head shines faintly, but it is his piercing eyes that hold attention: cold, calculating, and dangerous. His dark, finely cut clothes speak of wealth, and his predatory stillness of violence and control. Before him stands an uncorked bottle of rare Hammerdeep whiskey, untouched, as if daring anyone to drink. At his elbow lies the map case from the auction, shut.

{actor: Silverhand} Without standing, Silverhand locks his gaze on the adventurers as they step into the room.

{speaker: Silverhand, delivery: cold and accusing} You’re late.

{actor: Silverhand} He leans forward, his silver-clad fingers tapping the table with a metallic clink.

{speaker: Silverhand} I don’t like being followed. I like being interrupted even less.

{actor: Silverhand} He gestures lazily toward the empty chairs. It is a command, not an offer.

{speaker: Silverhand} Sit. You’ve gone through a lot of trouble to find me, but let’s be clear.

{actor: Silverhand} His silver gauntlet flexes with unsettling precision.

{speaker: Silverhand, delivery: dropping to a growl} This is my domain. You are here because I have allowed it, and you leave when I decide.

{actor: Silverhand} He pauses, eyes narrowing.

{speaker: Silverhand} Now, tell me why I shouldn’t have you thrown into the harbor, or worse.

## GM Notes

This encounter could play itself out in a variety of ways.

If the party was dragged in as captives, they come to in chairs at the table with Varchk behind them, unbound, and Silverhand talks to them all the same.

Silverhand is an alias used by Kazimir Raskov, the head of the Raskov Clan’s smuggling operations. He was in fact behind the operation to steal the Blackthorn shipment at the docks. However, he was double crossed by one of his men who was secretly a member of an organization known as the Covenant of the Cowl. 

**Secret: the man who crossed him was Joran Antonov.** Some weeks ago Joran joined Silverhand's crew under a false name, Lev, to get at the shipment and the Key of Ilmarin in it. Silverhand never learned who he really was. Nothing in this scene tells the party, and no one here hints at it. The truth comes out only at the final confrontation.

**What Silverhand tells, if asked:**
- The man called himself Lev, a quiet young Valkaran he took on a few weeks ago. Good with locks and letters, and kept to himself. No one vouched for him. He walked in asking for work, kept his hood up, and Silverhand describes him no closer than that.
- His crew opened the Blackthorn shipment on the quay for one piece, an old Valkaran key. Lev had it in his hands, and then he and the key were gone. The alarm went up, and the Blackthorns took back the rest of the cargo. He gives no warehouse or place for it. The key was old dark iron with worn runes he could not read.
- He does not know where Lev went. His people have searched since and found nothing, and he believes the Cowl hides its own. He names no street, house, grate or tunnel, and nothing here leads anywhere but on to The Ambush.
- Only word of Lev or the Cowl interests him. Maps, tunnels, cellars and services do not, and he says so. He gives each fact here once when asked and knows nothing past them.
- He has never heard the name Joran Antonov. Told that Joran was seen meeting him, he shrugs: he meets many men, none by that name. He does not connect Joran with Lev.

**The map case.** The case on the table holds the map his man won at the auction, a relic of Old Valkara. It shows a route into the Valkarr Forest to a lost cache marked deep in the wood. It did not come off the Blackthorn shipment, and it has nothing to do with the Key of Ilmarin or the Cowl. Asked about it, he says once: "A way out of this city, and none of your business." He does not open it or let anyone read it. A hero who glimpses it sees old forest trails and one mark, nothing more. Neither map nor case carries magic.

**A grab or a threat.** Silverhand does not fight and does not let the party fight him here. A hero who seizes the case or draws steel finds Varchk at their shoulder and two of Silverhand's men stepping from behind the crates. Silverhand says once: "Put it down and walk out, or swim." The case goes back on the table, and the meeting is over that round.

Kazimir wishes to avoid any direct confrontation with the Covenant. His instincts tell him that it is too dangerous. They seem to know everything, with spies operating everywhere. In fact, he is considering leaving the city to expand their operations elsewhere.

**A hero he owes.** If a hero came to Kordavos to collect coin Silverhand owes them, he remembers it as an old job and does not deny the debt. He pays nothing tonight. He offers to clear it in full for word of Lev, and says so once.

**Ending the meeting.** Once the party has asked its questions, and by the end of the fourth round at the latest, Silverhand ends it once: "We're done. Varchk, see them out." Varchk walks them to the door, and the night air takes them back toward the inn.
