## Intro

In the grand ballroom the mood has turned to eager anticipation. Warm light flickers over the elegantly dressed guests. Thaddeus Blackthorn stands confidently on the stage, his dark formal attire gleaming under the chandeliers. He raises his hand with a proud smirk, and the room falls silent.

“Ladies and gentlemen,” Thaddeus begins, his voice booming with practiced ease. “We stand on the brink of greatness. A new era of prosperity awaits those bold enough to embrace it. Those who align themselves with the Blackthorns and the Margrave will find opportunity and wealth. But,” he pauses, his gaze sweeping the room, “those who cling to outdated traditions will be left behind. The future belongs to those who dare to seize it.”

As the crowd murmurs approval, Thaddeus gestures to the far side of the ballroom. With a flourish, a large curtain is drawn back to reveal the Forest Drake, caged but still impressive in size and power. Its green scales shimmer, and its bright eyes survey the crowd with predatory focus. "Behold," Thaddeus announces, "a symbol of the new alliances we've forged with the elves of the Valkarr Forest. Together, we will unlock the ancient secrets of the wilds and bring forth untold riches and power!"

The crowd gasps in awe as the drake towers above them.

## GM Notes

If the Forest Drake is unavailable, the half-elf Eamon steps forward, his hands weaving intricate patterns in the air. A moment later, an illusion of the Forest Drake takes form—so realistic it almost seems to breathe.
