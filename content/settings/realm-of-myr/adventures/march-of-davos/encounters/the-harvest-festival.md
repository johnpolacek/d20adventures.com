## Intro

Beyond the gates, the annual Valkaran Harvest Festival fills the streets. Stalls show crafts and goods from all over Thylún. Jugglers, acrobats, and fire-eaters compete for the crowd's attention, and the smell of roasted meats, sweet pastries, and spiced wine draws people to the food stalls.

{place: gameStall} "Come, try your luck!" a vendor shouts, waving at a game of chance with small trinkets and toys for prizes.

{actor: madam-zephyra} At another stall the fortune teller Madam Zephyra peers into a crystal ball, her face half hidden by a veil.

{speaker: madam-zephyra, delivery: whispering with a mysterious smile} I see great things in your future.

{place: musicStage} On a stage in the center of the square, musicians play lively Valkaran tunes and dancers invite onlookers to join in. Children dart through the crowd, laughing and playing. The celebration is hard to resist.

## GM Notes

- The heroes met at the gate and came through as one company, each answering to the watch for the others. This is their first free hour together. Let them learn about each other here: where each is from, what brought them to Kordavos, what they make of the city. A hero who has not heard another's name may ask it now.
- **Pace.** Two rounds. Keep the rounds short: the heroes lead, and the vendors answer.
- **The vendors wait to be addressed.** Each vendor or performer acts only on a turn when a hero has spoken to them or come to their stall. Otherwise they skip the turn with nothing told. No vendor calls to the heroes unasked after the intro. No one repeats an offer, a riddle or a question, and no one asks a name they have already heard.
- Use these talks to show the tension between the Valkaran natives and the Asterian Empire.
- **Karim (Jewel Merchant)**: Openly favors the Blackthorns for their 'modern' sensibilities. His best stone is a blue sapphire, new-cut in the Asterian style, for 40 gold. The price is fixed, and no roll lowers it. Asked where his stones come from, he says Asterian mines by way of Blackthorn traders, and no more. He watches his trays. A hero who tries to palm a stone rolls Sleight of Hand, DC 16. A success takes a small chip of garnet worth 2 gold. A failure gets the tray pulled back and a cold warning, and Karim deals no more with that hero. No watch is called.
- **Liora (Spice Merchant)**: A traditionalist who grumbles about how Asterians are diluting Valkaran culture.
- **Madam Zephyra**: Gives cryptic hints about "keys to forgotten rooms" or "shadows beneath the mansion" to foreshadow later scenes. Her two riddles are in her behavior. Each is told once.
- **The interruption.** At the end of the second round, a furious shout from the edge of the square cuts across the music, and the crowd turns toward it. Tell it in a sentence or two. That ends the scene.

## Transitions

- To [[encounter:clan-conflict]] when the second round ends. A shout from the edge of the square draws the crowd and the heroes toward a quarrel between two clans.
