## Intro

Beyond the gates, the annual Valkaran Harvest Festival fills the streets. Stalls show crafts and goods from all over Thylún. Jugglers, acrobats, and fire-eaters compete for the crowd's attention, and the smell of roasted meats, sweet pastries, and spiced wine draws people to the food stalls.

{place: gameStall} "Come, try your luck!" a vendor shouts, waving at a game of chance with small trinkets and toys for prizes.

{actor: madam-zephyra} At another stall the fortune teller Madam Zephyra peers into a crystal ball, her face half hidden by a veil.

{speaker: madam-zephyra, delivery: whispering with a mysterious smile} I see great things in your future.

{place: musicStage} On a stage in the center of the square, musicians play lively Valkaran tunes and dancers invite onlookers to join in. Children dart through the crowd, laughing and playing. The celebration is hard to resist.

## GM Notes

- The heroes met at the gate and came through as one company, each answering to the watch for the others. This is their first free hour together. Let them learn about each other here: where each is from, what brought them to Kordavos, what they make of the city. A hero who has not heard another's name may ask it now.
- Characters can interact with vendors and performers. Use these interactions to establish the cultural tension between the Valkaran natives and the Asterian Empire.
- **Karim (Jewel Merchant)**: Openly favors the Blackthorns for their 'modern' sensibilities.
- **Liora (Spice Merchant)**: A traditionalist who grumbles about how Asterians are diluting Valkaran culture.
- **Madam Zephyra**: Can provide cryptic hints about "keys to forgotten rooms" or "shadows beneath the mansion" to foreshadow future plot points.

**Trigger:** Once the players have engaged with a few stalls or characters, transition to [[encounter:clan-conflict]] as a heated argument erupts on the edge of the square, drawing the attention of the crowd.
