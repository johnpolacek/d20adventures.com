## Intro

The party is ushered into a candlelit chamber deeper in the Black Market, quiet, dark, and secretive after the bustling stalls. Rows of velvet seats face a small raised platform where the items will be shown. Shady figures, wealthy nobles, and collectors fill the seats, their faces hidden behind ornate masks.

{actor: auctioneer} A tall, slim figure in a black robe and black leather mask strides onto the platform.

{speaker: auctioneer, delivery: smooth and commanding} Ladies and gentlemen, esteemed guests, welcome to tonight’s exclusive event. We have gathered some of the most coveted items in the realm. May you bid wisely… and generously.

{actor: auctioneer} The auctioneer gestures, and an assistant unveils the first item, a jeweled dagger glinting in the dim light. The crowd stirs as the bidding begins.

## GM Notes

If the characters look for Silverhand, they can find him on a successful perception check.

Seated near the back, yet still commanding an undeniable presence, he exudes an air of cold calculation. His tall, slender frame is cloaked in a dark, richly embroidered coat. His left hand, encased in an intricate gauntlet of polished silver, rests on the arm of his chair. Beneath his hood, only the lower half of his face is visible, showing a thin-lipped smirk that hints at both intelligence and ruthlessness. Though his face remains shadowed, everyone in the room knows who he is: the man who controls the flow of illicit goods and information in Kordavos.

**The lots, in this order.** A sold lot is never offered again, and no other lots are invented.
1. A jeweled dagger. Opens at 50 gold and sells for 120 to a masked noblewoman with a lacquered fan, unless a hero outbids her.
2. A silver-chased hunting horn. Opens at 40 gold and sells for 60.
3. The relic of Old Valkara, a map. It is the next scene.

The dagger and horn are fine work and nothing more. Detect Magic finds no aura on them or on Silverhand's gauntlet, and Detect Evil and Good finds only mortals in the room.

A hero may bid on the dagger or horn with coin they carry. The auctioneer asks to see it before the gavel falls.

Move on once the party has spotted Silverhand or both lots have sold, at the latest at the end of the second round. The auctioneer then calls the third lot.
