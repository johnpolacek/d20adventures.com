## Intro

Dawn breaks over Kordavos to the sound of bells. In the night the Asterian banners came down from the gate towers, and the old symbols of Valkara hang in their place.

From the balcony of the Blackthorn mansion, Joran Antonov declares the city free, the Key of Ilmarin raised in his hand. Below him the square fills with Valkarans who have not dared to gather in the old colors for a generation. Seraphina Valtor stands at his shoulder, already speaking quietly with the clan elders about who will sit on the new council.

Not everyone cheers. Blackthorn mercenaries and Asterian loyalists have barred themselves in the counting houses by the river, and the word in the streets is that they mean to flee with the city's treasury before the gates close.

The March of Davos has chosen its old ways. Whether it can hold them is a story for another day.

## GM Notes

The adventurers sided with Joran and the Covenant of the Cowl. This is the end of March of Davos: the Valkaran Restoration.

If every hero died in the inner sanctum, they are not here. Joran raises the Key alone, and the heroes are mourned among the fallen.
