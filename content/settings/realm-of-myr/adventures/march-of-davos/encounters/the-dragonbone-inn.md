## Intro

The sun sets in streaks of orange and purple, and it is time to find lodging for the night. The closest tavern is The Dragonbone Inn, known for its eclectic patrons and storied history. It promises a welcome rest from the road.

Inside it is warm and smells of roasting meat, and a bard plays a jaunty tune on his lute. Merchants and travelers fill the room, with a few suspicious figures huddled in dark corners. A large fireplace dominates one wall, its flames casting dancing shadows.

{actor: bram} The innkeeper, a burly man named Bram, greets the adventurers heartily and ushers them to a corner table where they can talk in relative privacy. He sets down a tray of drinks.

{speaker: bram, delivery: hearty} Ah, the road can be a harsh mistress, can't she? You lot look like you've seen some excitement. This corner will give you a bit of peace.

## GM Notes

The heroes take lodging for the night here. They can talk over the festival and the clans' quarrel, and get to know each other. Allow some roleplay between the characters.

**Pace.** One round. At its end, a scarred dockworker and a spectacled scholar by the bar, who have been watching the heroes' table, come over. Tell it in a sentence.

**Bram's prices.** These do not change. Bram says them once, when asked or when a hero pays. He does not haggle, so a hero who offers less gets the coin slid back with the price said again, and no roll.
- A bed upstairs: 2 silver a head for the night, with supper and breakfast.
- Ale: 2 copper a mug. Wine: 5 copper a cup. A bowl of stew: 3 copper.
- The first tray of drinks is on the house, for the company that came through the Festival in one piece.

**Bram** knows the city's gossip but no secrets. He has heard of the clans' quarrel and says the Festival brings out the worst in the Blackthorns and the Valtors. The figures in the dark corners are dock smugglers and card sharps. Bram knows them by face, not name, keeps out of their business, and says so.

## Transitions
