## Intro

The Key of Ilmarin rests now in the vaults of the Church of Valkara, watched by Sister Danica and the Wayfinders, and neither house can reach it.

With the Key beyond them both, the Blackthorns and the Valtors come to the table in the Old Grand Library. Bishop Nicolai presides, and the talks run long into the nights. No one trusts anyone, but no one dares to strike first.

Kordavos settles into a cold and watchful peace. The festival banners come down, the river barges sail again, and the Raskovs quietly go back to business.

The March of Davos has chosen to wait. How long the truce can hold is a story for another day.

## GM Notes

The adventurers took neither side and put the Key in the keeping of the Church. This is the end of March of Davos: the Wayfinder's Truce.
