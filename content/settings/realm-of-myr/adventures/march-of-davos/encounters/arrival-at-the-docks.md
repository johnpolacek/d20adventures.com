## Intro

The adventurers arrive at the bustling docks of Kordavos in golden sunlight. The air smells of saltwater and freshly unloaded cargo. Tall ships bob in their berths, and dockworkers hauling crates and barrels shout to one another over the gulls and the waves against the piers.

{actor: loric-windrider} Ahead, a group of dockworkers pause and turn their sweat-streaked faces toward the adventurers. One of them, a grizzled veteran with a sun-worn face and a heavy leather apron, sets down a crate, wipes his brow, and eyes them warily.

## GM Notes

The goal here is to find information about the missing shipment or Joran. The dockworkers are wary of the city guard and noble families, so characters should use **Persuasion**, **Intimidation**, or perhaps a bribe to get them talking.

### Finding Loric

The grizzled veteran is **Loric Windrider**. He is the foreman of this section of the docks. If approached respectfully, or if the players mention Elara, he may open up.

**Checks:**
- **DC 12 Insight:** Players notice Loric is glancing nervously toward a smaller, guarded warehouse down the pier.
- **DC 15 Persuasion:** Loric admits he saw a strange shipment from Faelendar (the elven settlement) being diverted to a private shed rather than the main Blackthorn warehouse.
- **DC 12 Persuasion, or a few coins:** Loric mentions a river tug that came down the Mordava at first light a day or two back, low in the water for a single chained crate. Two Blackthorn men brought it in, a big soldier and a lean, quiet one the others called Silas. That crate went to Warehouse 14 as well. If the story so far says the tug never left the pier or lost its crate there, Loric has seen no such tug, and Blackthorn men have been asking the docks after one that never came.

### Loric's Tale

Loric lowers his voice, leaning in. "That shipment you're asking about? It didn't go where it was supposed to. A few of the Blackthorn's 'specialists'—rough types, not regular dockhands—moved it to Warehouse 14. They seemed... anxious. Like they were hiding something more than just elven silk."

He points a calloused finger toward an inconspicuous storage shed tucked away behind larger buildings. "They're still there. Watching. Best be careful."

**What the docks know.** Loric and his crew know only these.
- Silverhand is a smuggler who works the Black Market in the Lower Districts. He has not been seen on these piers.
- None of them know Joran Antonov.
- Cowl-wearers have come round at night asking after the shipment. Loric sent them off.
- No watch patrols this end of the quay.

**Warehouse 14.** Four men in dockhand clothes idle by its door, too alert for dockhands. They are Alaric and his three blades. Nothing can be learned of the shed from outside. A hero who sneaks up or creates a distraction reaches the door with the rest of the party, and the watchers are met in the next scene. A hero who walks up to them openly, to talk or to threaten, brings the whole party to the door: close the scene at that round's end with no word from the watchers, who answer in the next scene.

**Moving on early.** The street to the Lower Districts runs along this quay. A hero who heads for it, or for anywhere else, does not leave the docks in this scene. The press of porters slows them, and the others catch up. Loric and his crew never send the party away from the docks or toward the Lower Districts. A hero who turns away before Loric names the shed hears it from Petra as they pass: "Ask at Warehouse 14 first. Whatever you're after came through there."

**Pace.** Once Loric names Warehouse 14, or the second round ends, close the scene at that round's end with the party heading for the shed together.
