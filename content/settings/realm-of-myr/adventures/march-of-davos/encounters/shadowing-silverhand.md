## Intro

Late evening falls over Kordavos as the adventurers emerge from the underground chamber. A low moon casts a dim, silvery glow over the streets. Ahead, Silverhand strides purposefully through the gloom, his cloak rippling like a living shadow. His representative follows, clutching the auction prize tightly.

The hum of the city’s nightlife fades as the trail winds through the alleys, under flickering lanterns that barely light the way. Silverhand leads deeper into the city’s forgotten corners, far from the eyes of the law. Every few intersections, one of his guards pauses to glance back for tails.

## GM Notes

Silverhand’s people are seasoned professionals, and following them undetected will require a group stealth or deception check. If they fail, the group is quickly noticed, and two of Silverhand’s guards subtly block their path in a narrow alley. The guards won’t initiate combat right away, but will issue a stern warning to "mind their own business" and leave. If the party presses, they can try persuasion or intimidation to continue the pursuit, but pushing too hard risks provoking a fight.

If they just attack, other Silverhand bodyguards will appear quickly to defend him. The guards beat attackers down with clubs and do not kill. Downed heroes come to in the alley as Silverhand slips away toward the docks, and the trail is easy to pick up again.

No one takes the map case in this scene. A hero who goes for it meets Varchk's shoulder while the representative runs ahead with it.

Silverhand does not talk with the adventurers in this scene, and does not bring them along. He keeps walking. If they catch up with him or block his way, his guards close in and he slips off into the mist toward the docks. His meeting with them comes at his hideout, the next scene, where he lets them find him.

The party trails Silverhand together. A hero who goes ahead alone is turned back by a guard in the alley and falls in with the others again before the hideout.

**If they will not follow.** A party that turns away or heads for the inn is overtaken by Kaeleth, who says once: "Silverhand would rather meet you than be followed. The warehouse at the end of the docks." She leads them there.

Move on at the end of the third round at the latest, as the trail ends at the docks.
