## Intro

The seal dissolves with a deafening crack, and the black iron door rumbles open. The adventurers brace for an attack, but find only... silence.

Beyond lies a vast, cavernous chamber, its ceiling lost in darkness the torches cannot reach. Fine grey dust covers the floor. It looks like a tomb picked clean. At the far end, a single narrow archway leads further in.

{actor: astrid-blackthorn} Astrid steps into the center of the hall, her voice echoing.

{speaker: astrid-blackthorn, delivery: frustrated} All this? For an empty cellar?

{actor: astrid-blackthorn} She kicks at the dust.

{actor: eamon} Eamon stays back, eyes on the floor.

{speaker: eamon, to: astrid-blackthorn, delivery: uneasy} Astrid, wait... look at the dust. There are no footprints leading to that archway. Only scuff marks beneath the dust.

## GM Notes

**Environmental Cues:**
As Eamon speaks, the temperature drops further. The air grows thick with a supernatural chill, signaling a presence far older than the ruins themselves. Shadows seem to flicker at the edge of vision, suggesting the room is not as empty as it first appeared.

Then the Ancient Undead rise from the dust and attack. If the seal was broken badly at the sealed door, they have surprise. Astrid and Eamon fight beside the party. Once the undead fall, the way to the archway is clear.
