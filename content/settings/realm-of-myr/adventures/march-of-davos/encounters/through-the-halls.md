## Intro

A dimly lit hallway stretches ahead, a distant candle casting long shadows on the stone walls. The noise of the ball fades behind, replaced by the creak of wood underfoot.

The hall ends at two stairways. On the left, a spiral staircase with polished, ornate bannisters rises to the upper floors, hinting at more luxurious chambers. Above, one passage leads to the family rooms and another to the master's wing. On the right, narrow steps descend into the bowels of the mansion, where the air is cooler and smells faintly of aged wine or damp stone.

## GM Notes

**One way at a time.** The heroes choose one way together: up to the family rooms, up to the master's wing, down to the cellar, or back to the ballroom. Do not split the party. If the heroes propose splitting up, the nearness of the guards and the shortness of the song argue against it, and they go together to whichever way the most of them chose. A hero who goes off alone anyway is turned back by a passing servant and rejoins the others before the next room.

**The song.** Aelindor's song does not wait. If the party is still here at the end of the second round, the last note fades below, applause swells, and Thaddeus takes the stage. The party hurries back down to the ballroom and slips into the crowd as he begins. If the party has already searched two rooms since the song began, it ends at the end of the first round here instead.

**Caught.** If the house guard has the adventurers in hand, the guards do not march them about the house or search it with them. They take them straight down to the ballroom to answer to Lady Astrid, and arrive just as Thaddeus takes the stage. Every eye turns to him, and the guards hold the adventurers at the edge of the crowd while he speaks.
