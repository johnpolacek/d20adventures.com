## Intro

As the applause for Thaddeus’ announcement peaks, a figure in the shadows moves with purpose. Clad in the dark, hooded garb of the Covenant of the Cowl, the assassin has waited for this moment, while every eye is on the Forest Drake.

{actor: covenant-assassin, target: thaddeus-blackthorn, object: blade} With a sudden, calculated movement, the assassin breaks from the crowd near Thaddeus and plunges a blade into his side.

{speaker: covenant-assassin, to: thaddeus-blackthorn, delivery: hissing} Death to the murderer of Mikhail Antonov!

{actor: thaddeus-blackthorn} Thaddeus gasps, eyes wide in shock, as the poison begins to work through his veins.

Chaos erupts. Guests scream and scatter.

{actor: covenant-assassin} The assassin races for the balcony with deadly speed and vaults the bannister, a rope ready for a quick descent into the darkness below.

## GM Notes

The players have a decision to make. They could give chase, or go to Thaddeus. If they go to Thaddeus, it is possible they could obtain his key. Regardless, there is nothing they can do to help him—he will die.

The balcony overlooks the walled Blackthorn garden, not the street. The assassin's rope drops him among the hedges below, and once down he is out of sight in the dark garden, making for the grate. He never reaches the festival streets.

Nothing in this scene catches, slows or wounds him. A spell or blow aimed after him finds only the swinging rope and dark hedges. A hero who climbs down lands in an empty garden, and the chase begins there.

Thaddeus's key hangs at his belt: black iron with an onyx head, faintly enchanted. It opens his desk and the basement vault. A hero at his side in the first round may take it unseen with a DC 12 Sleight of Hand check. Otherwise Astrid takes it from his belt when she reaches him and keeps it in her closed hand. No one takes it from her.

The blade was coated with a fast grey poison: the wound blackens at its edges and grey threads spread under the skin. A hero who studies it learns no more than that, and no name or maker.

Thaddeus speaks once, asking for Astrid, and then dies. He speaks no line after his death, and Astrid's name when she arrives is said by narration or a guest, not by him. Thaddeus dies at the end of the first round. Healing, spells and potions slow nothing. Astrid kneels at his side as he dies, and house guards close every stair and door into the house. No one gets back upstairs or down to the cellar tonight, so the key opens nothing during the ball.

The Covenant blames Thaddeus, who as head of the house ordered Mikhail's death. Astrid carried it out. The journal in her quarters reveals this, and Joran confronts her with it at the end.
