## Intro

Past the detonation point, the tunnel branches into a confusing web of intersections. Each fork looks identical: rough-hewn stone, dripping water, and no natural light. The air grows heavier, carrying a faint, pulsing hum that vibrates in the teeth of the magically sensitive.

“Too many turns,” Astrid mutters, her brow furrowed. “It’s designed to lose anyone who isn’t supposed to be down here.” She glances back at her small squad of surviving soldiers. “Split up. Take the east and west spurs. Report back if you find anything. We will take the center path.”

As you press on with Astrid and Eamon, the scholar pauses to run his fingers along faint markings on the walls—crystal-embedded runes that hum with a faint, resonant frequency. “These aren’t just markings,” Eamon whispers. “They’re transmission stones. The Covenant has been using these tunnels to eavesdrop on the noble estates above. Look at the angle—this one is directly beneath the City Council chambers.”

After a sharp turn, the tunnel ends abruptly at a massive, seamless stone wall. The shadows seem to press in closer, and the hum of power reaches a crescendo.

## GM Notes

**The Surveillance Network:**
If players spend time investigating the runes (**DC 15 Investigation**), they realize the Covenant has been manipulating the city's politics for years, not just months. This adds weight to the final choice: the Covenant isn't just a religious sect; they are a deep-state intelligence agency for the Valtors.

**Finding the Way:**
Eamon can track the "core center of power" with his magic, but he needs the party's protection as the tunnel walls themselves begin to shift slightly—an illusory defense mechanism.
