## Intro

{place: gateArch} The adventurers approach the grand gates of Kordavos, towering stone archways hung with Asterian and Valkaran banners that flutter in the breeze. Laughter, music and chatter carry from the Harvest Festival just beyond.

{place: lineBack} Before they can join the revelry, they must wait in a long line of travelers, merchants and villagers to be inspected by the city guard. A few guards move through the crowd, pulling people aside for questioning.

{actor: garlan-ironfist} At a table beneath the arch, a burly Asterian sergeant with a stern face but kind eyes rises and raises a hand for quiet.

{speaker: garlan-ironfist, delivery: shouting} Festival law! No one passes this gate alone. Travelers enter as a company, every name written in the gate book together, and each of you answers for the others while you're in the city.

Grumbling runs down the line. Families and merchant trains close ranks and are waved through, until only those who came alone are left at the front, sizing one another up.

{hero: branka-stoneveil} A broad dwarf with copper braids and a twice-broken nose stares up at the old gate-ward sigil carved over the arch. The same sigil is stamped on her battered breastplate, and two of the guards have noticed.

{hero: cassia-verane} A precise Asterian woman in a grey coat lined with map pockets has a brass surveyor's glass trained on the gatehouse footings. She sketches in a thick folio, frowning as if the stones are older than they should be.

{hero: yeva-softstep} A small halfling in a festival cap has chatted her way three places up the line. She is cheerful with everyone, and her quick hazel eyes never stop counting the guards.

{hero: milos-radan} A grey-bearded priest in undyed robes, the bronze compass-star of the Church of Valkara at his chest, shares water from his gourd with a weary pilgrim family before they pass.

{hero: wrenna-faelendar} A tall elf in grey-green leathers carries a bundle of furs like a trader, yet her pale yew longbow is strung, and she watches the guards the way a scout watches a treeline.

{hero: ilya-veles} A half-elf in an embroidered festival coat plucks a few bars on the gusle across his back, and half the line hums along. His grey eyes move from face to face, as if each stranger were a verse.

{hero: thalbern} A lean, weathered man in a cloak of forest hues stands a little apart, longbow across his back. River mud still cakes his boots, and he keeps glancing back down the road toward the Mordava.

{hero: 1749159962941} A young scholar in spectacles keeps her hood up. The hem of her robe is stained with river water, and she peels apart the swollen pages of a book, looking up sharply whenever a guard passes.

{hero: 1749307435667} A halfling in a patchwork waistcoat works the line for gossip, trading a joke for every rumor, his bright hazel eyes darting toward the city as if he has lost something in it.

{actor: garlan-ironfist} The sergeant opens the gate book and looks over the strangers before his table.

{speaker: garlan-ironfist, delivery: demanding} Next. Name and business in Kordavos, each of you, and three marks a head. Then tell me who stands with you.

## GM Notes

This scene is where the heroes meet. It succeeds when every hero has been seen, has said who they are in their own words, and goes through the gate with the others as one company. Play it in two or three rounds. Garlan runs the table, but the scene belongs to the heroes. Give them room to talk to each other.

**Festival law.** Last year's Festival ended in a brawl between the clans, so the governor revived an old Valkaran custom of surety. During the Festival no traveler enters alone. Travelers enter as a company, their names written together in the gate book, and each answers to the watch for what the others do in the city. Families and merchant trains pass at once. The heroes each came alone, so they must choose each other or wait outside until the Festival ends. The Stoneveils kept this custom when they were the gate-wards, and Branka knows it as her great-grandfather's law.

**How Garlan runs the table.**
- He calls the heroes up one at a time and asks each for a name and business. He says each name aloud as he writes it in the gate book, so the whole line hears it.
- He answers what each hero shows. He knows the Stoneveil name and the old gate-ward sigil, and treats Branka with gruff respect. He tells an elf with a strung bow to unstring it inside the walls. He asks the Wayfinder for a blessing on his watch. One of his guards thinks he has seen the halfling in the festival cap before, at some other Festival.
- A hero with no paragraph in the intro is shown when Garlan calls them up, with one detail of how they look.
- When every hero has given a name, he asks what to write for the company. Let the heroes name it. If they cannot agree, he writes "Garlan's Strays" and grins.
- He reads the company's names back aloud, takes the marks, and waves them through with a warning: the Blackthorns and the Valtors are spoiling for trouble this Festival, and a company that keeps its head down sees the end of it.

**Between the heroes.** Each hero shows something the others can notice and ask about: Branka's sigil, Cassia's charts, Yeva's counting eyes, Milos's compass-star, Wrenna's strung bow, Ilya's song, Thalbern's river mud, Lyra's soaked books, Poppen's questions. When one hero asks another about it, leave the answer to that hero. Never answer for them.

**Private business.** A hero may keep their errand from the others, or whisper it to Garlan. Keep a whisper private. Garlan keeps the land gate only. River cargo comes in at the docks, which he does not watch. Asked about a shipment off the river, he says to ask at the docks. He adds that Blackthorn wagons have been waved through his gate all week without inspection, on orders from above him.

**A hero who wants to go alone.** Garlan will not bend the law. They may wait outside until the Festival ends, or the others may talk them round. Do not hold the scene for it.
