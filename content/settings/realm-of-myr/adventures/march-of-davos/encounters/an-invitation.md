## Intro

{actor: astrid-blackthorn} Astrid studies the adventurers, a calculating glint in her forest-green eyes. From a pouch at her belt she draws a small envelope sealed with black wax and the Blackthorn crest.

{speaker: astrid-blackthorn} You have proven yourselves capable and, more importantly, brave. My family hosts a Masquerade Ball at our estate later this week, for the 'elite' of Kordavos. I believe your presence would be useful, and perhaps rewarding for you.

{actor: astrid-blackthorn, object: letter} She hands the adventurers the invitation.

{speaker: astrid-blackthorn} A token of my gratitude, and an opportunity. Rumor speaks of a 'Covenant' in the Lower Districts, and I suspect they are tied to the sabotage here. While we prepare for the ball, perhaps your... unique talents could learn what the common folk whisper in the shadows.

{actor: astrid-blackthorn} She nods.

{speaker: astrid-blackthorn} Come to the mansion at sunset on the festival's final day. Until then, stay sharp. Kordavos is rarely this loud without a reason.

## GM Notes

This marks the end of the initial investigation. The players now have a goal (The Masquerade) but also a lead (The Lower Districts/Covenant). This is the perfect time for the players to head toward the Black Market to follow up on the Silverhand lead.
