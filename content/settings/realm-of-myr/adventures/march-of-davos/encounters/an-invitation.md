## Intro

Astrid studies you, a calculating glint in her forest-green eyes. From a pouch at her belt she draws a small envelope sealed with black wax and the Blackthorn crest.

"You have proven yourselves capable and, more importantly, brave," she says. "My family hosts a Masquerade Ball at our estate later this week, for the 'elite' of Kordavos. I believe your presence would be useful, and perhaps rewarding for you."

She hands you the invitation. "A token of my gratitude, and an opportunity. Rumor speaks of a 'Covenant' in the Lower Districts, and I suspect they are tied to the sabotage here. While we prepare for the ball, perhaps your... unique talents could learn what the common folk whisper in the shadows."

She nods. "Come to the mansion at sunset on the festival's final day. Until then, stay sharp. Kordavos is rarely this loud without a reason."

## GM Notes

This marks the end of the initial investigation. The players now have a goal (The Masquerade) but also a lead (The Lower Districts/Covenant). This is the perfect time for the players to head toward the Black Market to follow up on the Silverhand lead.
