## Intro

Astrid’s gaze lingers on you, a calculating glint in her forest-green eyes. She reaches into a pouch at her belt and produces a small, elegant envelope sealed with thick black wax and the Blackthorn crest.

"You have proven yourselves capable and, more importantly, brave," she says. "My family is hosting a Masquerade Ball at our estate later this week. It is an event for the 'elite' of Kordavos, but I believe your presence would be beneficial—and perhaps rewarding for you."

She hands the invitation to you. "Consider it a token of my gratitude. But also an opportunity. There are rumors of a 'Covenant' operating in the Lower Districts, and I suspect they have ties to the sabotage here. While we prepare for the ball, perhaps you could use your... unique talents to see what the common folk are whispering about in the shadows."

She gives a final nod. "Come to the mansion when the sun sets on the final day of the festival. Until then, stay sharp. Kordavos is rarely this loud without a reason."

## GM Notes

This marks the end of the initial investigation. The players now have a goal (The Masquerade) but also a lead (The Lower Districts/Covenant). This is the perfect time for the players to head toward the Black Market to follow up on the Silverhand lead.
