## Intro

The gavel falls with a sharp crack. "Sold—to the gentleman in black!" the auctioneer proclaims, though his enthusiasm falters as the tension in the room thickens.

Without a word, Silverhand’s representative turns on his heel, slipping into the shadows with an air of purpose. A pair of silent guards fall in behind him, and together, they make their way toward the exit.
