## Intro

{actor: garlan-ironfist} At the table beneath the arch, a burly Asterian sergeant with a stern face but kind eyes sweeps the last company's marks into his strongbox. He turns the gate book to a fresh page and looks over the strangers now standing before him.

{speaker: garlan-ironfist, delivery: demanding} Next. Name and business in Kordavos, each of you, and three marks a head. Then tell me who stands with you.

## GM Notes

The heroes have met in the line. Now each says who they are to the gate. The scene succeeds when every hero has given a name, the heroes are written in the gate book as one company, and they go through the gate together. Garlan runs the table, but the scene belongs to the heroes. Let them vouch for each other with what they learned in the line.

**Pace.** Two rounds at most. The heroes have already chosen each other, so Garlan does not make them wait on him. Once every hero has given a name, he asks what to write for the company.
- A round ends with every name given, and the question not yet asked: the round's end has Garlan ask it, and the scene stays one more round for the heroes to answer.
- A round ends with the question asked and answered, or with the heroes unable to agree: close the scene in that round's end. Garlan writes the company's name, reads the names back, gives his warning and waves them through.
- The second round ends with anything still open: close the scene anyway. Garlan writes a hero with no name as below, writes "Garlan's Strays" if the company has no name, and waves them all through.

**Festival law.** No traveler enters alone during the Festival. Garlan writes the company's names together, and each answers to the watch for the others. See the gate line's notes for the custom's history.

**How Garlan runs the table.**
- He calls the heroes up one at a time and asks each for a name and business. He says each name aloud as he writes it in the gate book, so the whole line hears it.
- He answers what each hero shows. He knows the Stoneveil name and the old gate-ward sigil, and treats Branka with gruff respect. He tells an elf with a strung bow to unstring it inside the walls. He asks the Wayfinder for a blessing on his watch. He thinks he has seen the halfling in the festival cap before, at some other Festival, and says so himself. Any other hero he answers by the detail the line showed of them.
- When every hero has given a name, he asks what to write for the company. Let the heroes name it. If a hero offers a name and no hero objects, he writes that one. Only if they offer none, or cannot agree, does he write "Garlan's Strays" and grin.
- He reads the company's names back aloud, takes the marks, and waves them through with a warning: the Blackthorns and the Valtors are spoiling for trouble this Festival, and a company that keeps its head down sees the end of it.

**Between the heroes.** A hero may speak for another, or say what they made of them in the line. Leave each hero's own answers to that hero. Never answer for them.

**Private business.** A hero may keep their errand from the others, or whisper it to Garlan. Keep a whisper private. Garlan keeps the land gate only. River cargo comes in at the docks, which he does not watch. Asked about a shipment off the river, he says to ask at the docks. He adds that Blackthorn wagons have been waved through his gate all week without inspection, on orders from above him.

**A hero who refuses, walks off, or is left out.** No hero gets into the city alone, and no hero is left behind. Two guards at the arch turn back anyone who tries to pass without Garlan's wave, in a sentence. A hero who walks off is stopped there and stands at the arch until the company is written.
- Garlan writes no company while a hero in it is missing. He leaves a line open and asks the others who that hero is to them.
- The others may vouch: give the hero's name if they heard it, or a name the hero will answer to, and say they stand surety for them. Garlan accepts that, writes the hero on the company's line, and calls them back from the arch.
- If no one vouches and the hero still gives no name by the end of the second round, Garlan writes them as the others describe them, such as "the dwarf with the gate-ward sigil", says the company answers for them all the same, and waves the whole company through together.
- Never close the scene with a hero outside the gate book or outside the city.

## Transitions

- To [[encounter:the-harvest-festival]] when every hero is written in the gate book on one company's line, by their own name, a name the others vouched for, or Garlan's description, and Garlan has waved the whole company through the gate together.
