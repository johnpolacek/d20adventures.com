## Intro

The festival is in full swing with music and dance. On its outskirts, two groups stare each other down, and murmurs spread as the crowd senses the hostility.

{speaker: Barok Redbeard, to: Larya Halloway, delivery: shouting} Your people have dishonored us for the last time!

{actor: Barok Redbeard, target: Larya Halloway} His hand reaches for his sword.

{actor: Larya Halloway, target: Barok Redbeard} The other leader is a tall woman with a scar across her cheek.

{speaker: Larya Halloway, to: Barok Redbeard, delivery: snarling in response} And we won't tolerate your insults any longer!

{actor: Barok Redbeard, target: Larya Halloway} The first leader, a burly man with a red-dyed beard, steps forward. His followers, rough men and women in mismatched armor, move with him. His eyes lock onto the scarred woman.

{speaker: Barok Redbeard, to: Larya Halloway, delivery: growling} We've been patient, but your arrogance knows no bounds, Larya.

Larya's people, more finely dressed and some in intricate jewelry, hold their ground.

{speaker: Larya Halloway, to: Barok Redbeard, delivery: steady but filled with venom} It's you and your thugs who bring chaos wherever you go, Barok.

Onlookers inch away, leaving a circle around the two sides. Vendors glance about nervously, and parents pull their children close.

## GM Notes

The Valtor Clan, led by Larya, views the Blackthorn Clan member Barok and his followers as disrespectful intruders threatening their traditional way of life. They are offended at the presence of Asterian symbols and parade participants during the traditional Valkaran event.

Meanwhile, Barok and his group, who have embraced the Asterian way of life, aligning themselves with the Blackthorn clan, see the Valtor clan as archaic and obstructive. They have been accumulating power and wealth since the Asterians’ arrival and plan to continue to do so.

Unless the players intervene, the situation will escalate into a full blown fight requiring the city guard to intervene.

Depending on the actions of the adventurers, they may gain or lose favor with either or both factions, impacting future events in Kordavos.
