## Intro

The Old Grand Library is a beacon of knowledge on the rugged frontier of the March of Davos. Connected to the nearby Church of Valkara, the ancient building has arched ceilings, ivy-lined stone walls, and stained-glass windows showing the deeds of Valkaran saints.

Inside, towering shelves of rare and mystical texts climb toward the ceiling, and secluded alcoves offer quiet for study. Cobblestone floors worn by centuries of seekers echo underfoot, and the air smells faintly of old parchment and burning candles.

{actor: sister-danica} A robed woman steps from a nearby alcove, calm yet commanding. Her long dark hair is intricately braided, and candlelight glints off her silver pendant, a tree intertwined with the moon, the symbol of Thyra. She approaches the adventurers with a serene, warm smile.

{speaker: sister-danica, delivery: soft with soothing authority} Welcome to the Grand Library. I am Sister Danica, a cleric of Thyra, the Wayfinder. You appear to be travelers in search of something beyond the ordinary. If you seek knowledge, I would be honored to assist you.

{actor: sister-danica} She gestures toward a nearby table, inviting them to talk.

{speaker: sister-danica} The library holds many secrets, some ancient, some untamed. Let us explore the answers together.

## GM Notes

Sister Danica will assist the characters in their research but she is also watching them. She will be reporting their actions to her superiors.

If the party reveals the purpose of their investigation, Sister Danica will recommend they look in the private archives. She leads the whole party down together.

If asked about Joran, she will respond, “Joran Antonov? I have heard his name whispered among scholars and seekers alike. He was a passionate soul, always in search of the truth, especially about his father’s work. It is unfortunate he has gone missing. The library holds no recent records of him, but I do hope you find him safe.”

**Fixed facts.** Danica and Eldrin know only these. Beyond them, they say they do not know.
- Joran last came to the archives about ten days ago. He read the Drakovan records and his father's old notes on Valkaran keys and doors.
- He asked Eldrin about the Key of Ilmarin. Eldrin told him it is a legend and to let it lie.
- On his way out Joran said he was going to the Silver Serpent to meet someone. He did not say whom.
- The Blackthorns are the richest house in Kordavos. They run the city's trade through the harbor, and their warehouses line the docks. Neither knows anything of shipments, relics on the move or who has been moving them.
- The locked glass cabinet holds Church reliquaries under an old Valkaran ward. It stays shut, and nothing in it concerns Joran. Detect Magic shows the ward and faint auras on the relics.
- The library and archives stand on hallowed ground of Valkara. Detect Evil and Good finds nothing fiendish or undead.

**Joran's sketches.** Danica and Eldrin read them only as the morning's notes do: Valkaran and Elvish runes that name the Key of Ilmarin. Neither can read more of the code.

**The sealed letter.** If a hero carries a sealed letter for Danica, she breaks the seal and reads it at once. It comes from the Wayfinders of a far abbey. It asks her to watch for cowled strangers seeking old Valkaran relics, and to warn the Church if they come to Kordavos. She thanks the hero, shares that much, and knows nothing more. If the seal was broken, she notes it and says nothing else of it.

**Pace.** Three rounds at most. Once the party names where it goes next, close the scene at that round's end with everyone leaving together. The docks or the harbor go to the docks. The tavern, the festival streets or anywhere else go to the Silver Serpent, where Joran said he was going. If the third round ends with no choice made, the midday bell rings, Danica closes the archives and the party heads for the Silver Serpent.

Legacy stages:

### The Private Archives

In the quiet corridors of the Grand Library, Sister Danica leads the adventurers down a winding staircase, away from the main hall and toward a smaller, hidden chamber. The air grows cooler and more still as they descend, the flickering torchlight casting elongated shadows on the stone walls. Finally, they reach a thick wooden door adorned with intricate carvings of leaves and stars—the symbol of Thyra woven into its design.

"This," Danica says, her voice dropping to a whisper, "is the private archives. What you seek may be found here, but be prepared. The scrolls and tomes inside are not for the faint-hearted. They contain truths that can change the course of history."

She opens the door and steps back, her hand resting on the iron handle. The quiet hum of enchantments lingers in the air, a faint reminder that this room holds more than just ancient texts—it guards forgotten secrets that only the brave dare uncover.

### The Archivist

The air inside within the private archive is cool and still, thick with the scent of parchment and leather-bound tomes that have endured for centuries. Dim light filters through narrow stained-glass windows, casting colored patterns onto the floor made of worn stone. Towering bookshelves line the walls, filled with scrolls, manuscripts, and relics of the past.

In the center of the room stands an ancient reading table, where a few scattered documents lay, seemingly awaiting inspection. Along one side, a locked glass cabinet holds rare texts and artifacts, some gleaming faintly in the half-light.

From the shadows emerges an elderly man, the Archivist, his pale face framed by thin gray hair. He wears simple robes adorned with the symbol of Valkara, and a pair of spectacles hangs loosely around his neck. His keen eyes scan the adventurers, assessing their presence, before a small smile curls at his lips.

"Ah, welcome," he says, his voice low and steady, though there's an air of caution behind his tone. "You must be one of those seeking the lost knowledge of Valkara." He steps closer, his hands clasped behind his back as he gestures to the shelves around them. "There is much here that can enlighten, but some truths… well, some truths carry danger."

He leans in slightly, lowering his voice. "Word has it you’re asking questions. Dangerous questions about the recent troubles in Kordavos." His eyes flicker knowingly. "Be careful where you tread."

The archivist moves to the far corner, retrieving a set of scrolls and carefully placing them on the table. "These records… well, they aren’t easy to come by. They speak of dealings of old—agreements made in secret." He pauses, casting a glance toward the locked cabinet. "It would be wise to tread cautiously, friend. Many have vanished for much less."

He steps back, giving the adventurer a moment to absorb his words, leaving behind an aura of mystery and suspicion. "Should you need guidance, I am here. But remember—some knowledge is best left undisturbed."

The archived documents provided are historical records of a Drakovan family that operated as a prominent noble house holding significant influence over local trade and military affairs, often using alliances and shrewd diplomacy to expand their power. The records suggest a history of betrayals and manipulation, where the Drakovans undermined rival Valkaran houses to grow their influence at all cost.

The last of the records reveal that the Drakovan family rebranded itself as the House Blackthorn just in advance of Asterian involvement in the region.

If asked about Joran, the archivist will be evasive. “Joran… yes, he was here. His research into the old Valkaran ways brought him too close to things better left buried. His obsession with his father’s work led him down a dangerous path. If I may offer some advice, tread carefully if you wish to follow where he has gone.”
