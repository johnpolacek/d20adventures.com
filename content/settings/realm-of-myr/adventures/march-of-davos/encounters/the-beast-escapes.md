## Intro

You rush from the warehouse just in time to see a horrific scene. A nearby elven ship, the *Star of Lindrathil*, rocks violently in its berth. Startled cries fill the air as dockworkers dive into the water. A deep, guttural roar reverberates from the cargo hold—a sound of pure, ancient rage.

The thick wooden doors of the hold explode outward in a spray of splinters. A massive creature emerges, wings snapping open to catch the air. It is a **Forest Drake**, its scales shimmering in shades of mossy green and bark-brown. Its yellow eyes burn with a feral light as it lets out a deafening roar that rattles the windows of nearby shops.

At the edge of the gangplank, a woman stands with fierce determination. Tall and clad in rugged leather armor, her windblown black hair frames a face set in a mask of command. She draws a curved blade and shouts to the fleeing crew, “Keep it contained! If that beast reaches the lower district, it’ll be a massacre!” 

She spots you and gestures frantically. "You! If you can fight, help me!"

## GM Notes

This is a high-action combat encounter. The Forest Drake is confused and aggressive, attacking the nearest targets.

### Containing the Beast

- **The Forest Drake:** Uses its *Bite* and *Tail Slap*. It may attempt to fly up to the roofs of warehouses if pressured.
- **Astrid Blackthorn:** Fights alongside the players. She uses her bow to pin the creature's wings or distract it from vulnerable dockworkers.
- **Objective:** The players don't necessarily need to kill the drake; they can use chains, magic, or heavy nets found on the docks to restrain it. Astrid will prioritize capture over killing if possible.

### The Reveal

During the fight, characters with a **DC 14 Perception** notice the Drake's cage on the ship was not broken from the inside—the hinges look melted or corroded, suggesting intentional sabotage.
