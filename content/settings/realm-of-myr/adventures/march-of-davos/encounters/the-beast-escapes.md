## Intro

The adventurers rush from the warehouse. A nearby elven ship, the *Star of Faelendar*, rocks violently in its berth, and dockworkers dive into the water with startled cries. A deep, guttural roar of ancient rage rises from the cargo hold.

The hold's thick wooden doors burst outward in a spray of splinters. A massive **Forest Drake** emerges, wings snapping open, its scales mossy green and bark-brown. Its yellow eyes burn with a feral light, and its roar rattles the windows of nearby shops.

{actor: astrid-blackthorn, object: blade} At the edge of the gangplank stands a tall woman in rugged leather armor, her windblown black hair framing a commanding face. She draws a curved blade.

{speaker: astrid-blackthorn, delivery: shouting to the fleeing crew} Keep it contained! If that beast reaches the Lower Districts, it’ll be a massacre!

{actor: astrid-blackthorn} She spots the adventurers and gestures frantically.

{speaker: astrid-blackthorn} You! If you can fight, help me!

## GM Notes

This is a high-action combat encounter. The Forest Drake is confused and aggressive, attacking the nearest targets.

### Containing the Beast

- **The Forest Drake:** Uses its *Bite* and *Tail Slap*. It may attempt to fly up to the roofs of warehouses if pressured.
- **Astrid Blackthorn:** Fights alongside the players. She uses her bow to pin the creature's wings or distract it from vulnerable dockworkers.
- **Objective:** The players don't necessarily need to kill the drake; they can use chains, magic, or heavy nets found on the docks to restrain it. Astrid will prioritize capture over killing if possible.

**Checks.** The drake has no sheet. Every blow, net and spell against it lands only on a roll.
- A blade, arrow or bolt hits on DC 13. Three hits by heroes leave it bleeding and slowed, five beat it down.
- Heavy cargo nets lie by the crane. Throwing one over it is Athletics DC 13, and pinning a netted drake is Athletics DC 14. Two nets on it, or a net pinned, hold it.
- Chains, ropes or a spell such as Hold Monster or Web hold it on the spell's or check's success.
- A spell or trick that knocks it down, blinds it or fouls its footing, such as Grease, lets the next net land with no roll.
- Dockworkers caught on the quay run when a hero tells them to, no roll. Astrid shields the rest.
- The drake's bite or tail hits a hero on its attack roll against DC 13.
- If it takes to a roof, a hero reaches it by the crane or a stack of crates, Athletics DC 12. Arrows and spells reach it there. A net thrown up from the quay at a drake on a roof is Athletics DC 16.
- The hold of the *Star of Faelendar* holds the broken cage, straw and crates of elven silk and wine, and nothing worth stealing.

**Pace.** Three rounds at most. Astrid's arrows and nets count toward the hits and nets. If the drake is not held or beaten down by the end of the third round, it tears free, beats up over the roofs and flies north toward the Valkarr Forest. That counts as driven off. No one dies of a single blow from it.

**Astrid mid-fight.** Asked anything that is not about the drake, she says "Questions after." and answers nothing until the next scene.

### The Reveal

During the fight, characters with a **DC 14 Perception** notice the Drake's cage on the ship was not broken from the inside—the hinges look melted or corroded, suggesting intentional sabotage. The drake carries no enchantment or compulsion, and its chains are plain harbor iron.
