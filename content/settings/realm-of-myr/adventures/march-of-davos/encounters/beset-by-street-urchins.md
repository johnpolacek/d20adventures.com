## Intro

The winding streets of the Lower Districts teem with hurried vendors, wandering townsfolk, and shady figures lurking in the shadows. Chimney smoke hangs in the air, and the clatter of distant market stalls blends with the hum of conversation.

People jostle through the crowded, narrow alleys, faces hidden under hoods or wide-brimmed hats. Children dart between them, chasing each other or carrying small parcels, while street performers play haunting melodies on worn instruments.

Amid it all, a group of halfling urchins weaves unnoticed through the crowd with practiced ease. A small, wide-eyed halfling with messy brown hair slips between legs, laughing mischievously. A red-haired halfling with dirt-smudged cheeks skips along, bumping into people as she goes. More halflings trail behind, joking and giggling, lost in the bustle of the district.

## GM Notes

**The lift always happens.** In the first round, whatever the heroes say or do, Mara bumps into one hero and tries to lift Joran's scroll case. If no hero carries it, she goes for a purse or the best-looking trinket on the richest-looking hero. Talk, charm or a good Persuasion roll does not stop the attempt. It only changes whom she picks.
- A hero who said they were watching the children, or who passes a Perception check against DC 14, sees the hand and may grab it. Otherwise the item is gone, and a hero notices it missing at the end of that round.
- Finn Swiftfoot draws the eye with a tumble and a joke while Mara works.

**The chase.** The halflings dash into a narrow alley, laughing, and knock over crates and barrels behind them. They split up into different side streets. A hero who keeps up, by an Athletics or Acrobatics check against DC 13 or a spell, corners one of them.

**How it ends.** The scene settles it here. Nothing stolen reaches anyone later.
- A cornered urchin hands the item back and begs off. They know nothing of the Black Market's whereabouts and say so. Bystanders asked the way shrug and hurry off.
- If the thieves get clean away, the item is gone for good. A lost scroll case costs only Joran's sketches. The heroes still remember what Elara told them of the Key of Ilmarin.

Move on once the item is caught, recovered, or lost, at the latest at the end of the second round.
