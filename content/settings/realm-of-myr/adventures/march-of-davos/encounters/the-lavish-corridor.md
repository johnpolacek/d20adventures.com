## Intro

Deeper in the mansion’s winding halls is a wing clearly reserved for the highest-ranking member of the Blackthorn family. The decor grows ever more ornate: rich tapestries, priceless paintings of conquest and glory, and opulent candelabras casting a warm golden glow.

Two guards block the way to a large, intricately carved door. Both wear dark leather reinforced with iron plating, with the Blackthorn crest, a black tree twisted into a circle, stitched on their tabards.

They carry longswords, but they stand at ease, more interested in the Masquerade’s lavish spread than in any threat. The hall is otherwise silent, the ball barely audible this far into the mansion.

{actor: blackthorn-guard} One, a stocky man with graying hair, leans against the wall, absently fiddling with his armor straps. The other, a younger guard with a scruffy beard, chuckles and mutters about the wine and roasted game downstairs. Neither seems to care what goes on beyond the door they guard, though they still glance down the hall now and then.

## GM Notes

Unlike the highly alert soldiers in the main hall, these guards appear more complacent, their attention divided between their conversation and the events below. While they aren't entirely inattentive, they seem far more approachable and easier to influence.

**One party.** The heroes move as one group, and each room is played for all of them. If a hero goes off alone, a passing servant or a locked door turns them back, and they rejoin the others before the next room, having found nothing alone.

**The song.** Aelindor's song does not wait. At the end of the second round here, if the party has not left, the last note fades below, applause swells, and Thaddeus takes the stage. The party hurries back down to the ballroom and slips into the crowd as he begins. If the party has already searched two rooms since the song began, it ends at the end of the first round here instead.

**Caught.** If the house guard has the adventurers in hand, the guards do not march them about the house or search it with them. They take them straight down to the ballroom to answer to Lady Astrid, and arrive just as Thaddeus takes the stage. Every eye turns to him, and the guards hold the adventurers at the edge of the crowd while he speaks.

**Getting past.** One check for the party: Persuasion or Deception, DC 12, or a bribe of 5 gold or more to each guard, which they take with no roll. On a failure they send the party back to the ball with a laugh and no alarm, and one more try is allowed. Attacking them raises the alarm and the Caught rule follows. Thaddeus's door is locked: thieves' tools at DC 14, or a DC 16 Strength check that the guards hear if they are still there.

Friendly Conversation: A well-timed conversation or compliment about the Masquerade could distract them further, offering the party a chance to slip by unnoticed.

Small Bribe: These guards don’t seem too proud to accept a little "encouragement." A few gold coins or a rare drink from the ball might make them more inclined to turn a blind eye.

Convincing Story: Given their relaxed demeanor, the adventurers could easily convince them that Thaddeus had summoned them for an urgent discussion or delivery.

Magical Assistance: Given how at ease they are, even a simple illusion or Charm Person spell could sway them with little resistance.
