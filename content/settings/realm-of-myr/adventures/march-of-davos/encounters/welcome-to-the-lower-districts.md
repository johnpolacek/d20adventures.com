## Intro

Past the bustling docks, the streets of Kordavos give way to the shadowy alleys of the Lower Districts. The air grows heavy and stale, as if the sun rarely reaches between the narrow, leaning buildings. The laughter and commerce of the upper city fade. In their place come distant footsteps, whispered talk, and doors shut quickly as passersby hurry through the dim streets.

It is a different world, where the wealthy rarely tread and secrets are traded like coin. Dilapidated tenements loom on either side, their windows dark, and crooked alleys twist in every direction like a maze meant to lose even seasoned travelers. The uneven streets are slick with refuse, and the flicker of distant torches does little to pierce the gloom.

## GM Notes

Players typically arrive here after receiving an invitation and a task from Lady Astrid Blackthorn, or after following leads from the docks or the Silver Serpent Tavern. They have several potential motivations:

1.  **Find Silverhand**: They know that a smuggler named Silverhand (Kazimir Raskov) operates out of the Black Market here and was recently seen with Joran Antonov.
2.  **Investigate the Covenant**: Lady Astrid has tasked them with investigating the "Covenant of the Cowl," a group she suspects is responsible for recent sabotage.
3.  **Find Joran**: The missing brother was last seen heading toward this district to meet with dangerous elements.

The environment should feel hostile and secretive. NPCs are wary of outsiders and may require bribes or successful social checks to share information. This encounter is purely atmospheric and sets the stage for the pickpocketing incident in the next scene.
