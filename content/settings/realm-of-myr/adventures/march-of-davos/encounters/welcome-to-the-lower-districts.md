## Intro

Past the bustling docks, the streets of Kordavos give way to the shadowy alleys of the Lower Districts. The air grows heavy and stale, as if the sun rarely reaches between the narrow, leaning buildings. The laughter and commerce of the upper city fade. In their place come distant footsteps, whispered talk, and doors shut quickly as passersby hurry through the dim streets.

It is a different world, where the wealthy rarely tread and secrets are traded like coin. Dilapidated tenements loom on either side, their windows dark, and crooked alleys twist in every direction like a maze meant to lose even seasoned travelers. The uneven streets are slick with refuse, and the flicker of distant torches does little to pierce the gloom.

## GM Notes

Players arrive from the docks. They have met Lady Astrid, and unless they turned her down they carry her invitation and her ask to learn what the streets whisper about the Covenant. They may know the Key of Ilmarin is missing from the stolen shipment.

They may come to:
1. **Find Silverhand**: a smuggler who works the Black Market here and was seen with Joran Antonov.
2. **Learn of the cowl-wearers**: called the Covenant of the Cowl, if Astrid named them.
3. **Find Joran**: last seen heading here to meet dangerous people.

The streets feel hostile and secretive. Locals are wary of outsiders. For a bribe or a social check they grumble about the guard, the Raskovs and the noble houses, and say Silverhand is a name best not spoken. No one here tells where the Black Market is or how to get in. That is the next scenes' work. This scene is atmosphere and sets up the pickpockets in the next.

Move on once each hero has acted once, or at the end of the second round.
