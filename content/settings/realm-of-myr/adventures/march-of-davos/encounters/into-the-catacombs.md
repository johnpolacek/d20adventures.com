## Intro

A long, narrow tunnel runs deep into the foundations of Kordavos. The air is cool and damp, smelling faintly of iron and deep stone. For a while, only the echo of your footsteps breaks the silence.

After several minutes the walls widen. Torchlight flickers around a bend, and low, urgent voices echo from the chamber ahead.

Around the last corner, four hooded figures of the Covenant are feverishly setting six heavy wooden barrels against the support pillars. One kneels by a long alchemical fuse that snakes across the floor.

A voice mutters, “Hurry. The wards at the surface are down. Light it now! We’ll collapse the tunnel and bury the Blackthorn bitch with her guards.”

## GM Notes

This is a "Ticking Clock" encounter. The primary goal is to stop the explosion.

- **The Fuse:** The fuse will detonate at the end of the 3rd round. 
- **The Saboteurs:** 2 Covenant Rogues and 2 Cultists. They will prioritize defending the fuse-lighter.
- **Disarming:** A character can use an action to make a **DC 14 Sleight of Hand** or **Arcana** check to snuff the fuse. Using water or *Mage Hand* can also work creatively.

**Failure Scenarios:**
- If the barrels detonate, characters take 4d6 bludgeoning damage and must succeed on a **DC 15 Dexterity Save** to avoid being buried. The tunnel collapses, separating the party from Astrid's soldiers, who are forced to find another way around.

## Transitions

- To [[encounter:through-the-labyrinth]] once the threat of the explosion is resolved or the party flees deeper into the network.
