## Intro

Dawn light filters through the windows of the Dragonbone Inn, and the air smells of fresh bread and coffee. The innkeeper, Bram, is busy preparing for the day.

{actor: Elara Antonov} The door creaks open and Elara steps in, pale, tired, and visibly distraught.

{actor: bram, target: elara} Bram goes straight to her.

{speaker: bram, to: elara, delivery: concerned} Elara, you’re early today. Everything alright?

{actor: Elara Antonov} Elara gives him a faint smile.

{speaker: Elara Antonov, delivery: faltering} I’m here to drop off those supply orders... but...

{actor: bram} Bram glances at the adventurers, then back to her.

{speaker: bram, to: elara} You know, these folks here, they seem like the kind who could help you find Joran.

## GM Notes

**Elara's story, told once she sits with the party:**

Elara sinks into a chair near the adventurers. “My brother, Joran,” she whispers, “he’s always been one to chase dangerous truths. After our father’s death, he became obsessed with finding out why. He believed there was something more.”

“A few weeks ago, he learned of an artifact, a 'key' of some importance. Then one night he vanished. No note, no explanation. I’ve searched everywhere, but no one knows where he’s gone.”

She produces a small leather scrollcase. “This was Joran’s. He left these sketches and notes, but they’re in some sort of code. I can’t make sense of them.”

**What Elara knows.** These facts do not change. Beyond them, she says she does not know.
- Joran is twenty, dark-haired like her, thin, with ink always on his fingers.
- She last saw him at supper at home nine nights ago. He was gone by morning.
- He never said the name on his sketches. He spoke only of "a key". Shown the name Ilmarin, she says she has never heard it, from him or anyone.
- He never said whom he met at the Silver Serpent, and came home smelling of the river.
- She has no coin. Bram's free lodging is the only reward.

- **The Clues**: The scrollcase contains sketches of Valkaran runes and references to a "Key of Ilmarin." Successful Arcana or History checks reveal these are ancient Valkaran and Elvish symbols.
- **The Father**: Mikhail Antonov was a scholar murdered under mysterious circumstances. He discovered the location of a powerful artifact, which Joran was trying to find.
- **Bram** knows what he knew last night. The figures in the dark corners are dock smugglers and card sharps, known to him by face, not name. He knows Joran as Elara's quiet brother, who sometimes carried her orders in. He knows nothing of Joran's whereabouts or what he did.
- **A letter for Danica**: if a hero carries a sealed letter for Sister Danica, Bram or Elara tells them she keeps the Old Grand Library.
- **The Research**: Elara mentions Joran spent a lot of time at the **Old Grand Library** researching their father's work before he went to the **Silver Serpent Tavern**.
- **The Goal**: This provides the second major plot hook. Both the missing shipment (from the previous night) and the missing brother lead toward the docks and the library.
- **Bram's Incentive**: Bram will offer the party free lodging if they agree to help Elara.
- **Transition to Investigation**: This encounter is the final scene of the Arrival section. It sets the stakes for the next phase, where players must choose between scholarly research (The Library) or street-level inquiries (The Tavern/Docks).

**Pace.** Two rounds. Elara tells her story on her first turn. Once the heroes name the library or the tavern, close the scene at that round's end with the company setting off together. If the heroes name different places, the player's hero's choice wins. A hero who heads out alone is caught up by the others at the door. If the second round ends with no choice made, close the scene at the Silver Serpent, where both jobs point.

## Transitions

- To [[encounter:the-old-grand-library]] when the heroes choose to go to the library first.
- To [[encounter:the-silver-serpent-tavern]] when the heroes choose the tavern or the docks first, or the second round ends with no choice made.
