## Intro

As dawn breaks and pale light filters through the windows of the Dragonbone Inn, the smell of freshly baked bread and brewing coffee fills the air. The innkeeper, Bram, is busy preparing for the day. 

The door creaks open, and in steps Elara. She is visibly distraught, her face pale and tired. Bram immediately notices her and makes his way over. “Elara, you’re early today. Everything alright?”

Elara gives him a faint smile. “I’m here to drop off those supply orders... but...” Her voice falters. Bram looks at the adventurers and then back to her. “You know, these folks here,” he says, gesturing to the party, “they seem like the kind who could help you find Joran.”

## The Missing Brother

Elara sinks into a chair near the adventurers. “My brother, Joran,” she begins, her voice barely above a whisper, “he’s always been one to chase after dangerous truths. After our father’s death, he became obsessed with uncovering why. He believed there was something more.”

“A few weeks ago, he discovered information about an artifact—a 'key' of some importance. Then, one night, he vanished. No note, no explanation. I’ve searched everywhere, but no one knows where he’s gone.”

She produces a small leather scrollcase. “This belonged to Joran. He left behind these sketches and notes, but they’re written in some sort of code. I can't make sense of them.”

## GM Notes

- **The Clues**: The scrollcase contains sketches of Valkaran runes and references to a "Key of Ilmarin." Successful Arcana or History checks reveal these are ancient Valkaran and Elvish symbols.
- **The Father**: Milos Antonov was a scholar murdered under mysterious circumstances. He discovered the location of a powerful artifact, which Joran was trying to find.
- **The Research**: Elara mentions Joran spent a lot of time at the **Old Grand Library** researching their father's work before he went to the **Silver Serpent Tavern**.
- **The Goal**: This provides the second major plot hook. Both the missing shipment (from the previous night) and the missing brother lead toward the docks and the library.
- **Bram's Incentive**: Bram will offer the party free lodging if they agree to help Elara.
- **Transition to Investigation**: This encounter is the final scene of the Arrival section. It sets the stakes for the next phase, where players must choose between scholarly research (The Library) or street-level inquiries (The Tavern/Docks).
