## Intro

The dust settles on docks strewn with shattered crates and torn sails. The air smells of ozone and wet wood. Dockworkers creep from hiding, whispering in fear.

{actor: astrid-blackthorn} The woman ranger sheathes her blade and approaches. Up close, she is plainly noble despite the grime of battle. She wipes drake blood from her cheek and gives a stiff, respectful nod.

{speaker: astrid-blackthorn, delivery: her poise returning} I am Lady Astrid Blackthorn. It seems I am in your debt. Few in this city would run toward a drake while everyone else ran away. Well met.

{actor: petra-velan} Before the adventurers can answer, a dockworker named Petra calls from the ship’s deck.

{speaker: petra-velan, to: astrid-blackthorn, delivery: shouting} Astrid! Look at the cage! It wasn’t the beast! This was sabotage!

{actor: astrid-blackthorn} Astrid climbs aboard to inspect the cage’s melted steel, her face darkening.

{speaker: astrid-blackthorn, delivery: muttering} Acid. Someone wanted this creature loose. Someone wanted chaos for my family.

{actor: astrid-blackthorn} She turns to the adventurers.

{speaker: astrid-blackthorn} There is more at play here than simple theft.

## GM Notes

Astrid is impressed and sees the players as useful tools or allies. She will ask what they were doing at the docks. 

**Information to share:**
- If they mention the missing shipment, she confirms it was a Blackthorn delivery of "cultural artifacts."
- If they mention Silverhand, her jaw tightens. "The Raskovs are scavengers, but they usually aren't this bold."
- She suspects a third party is trying to drive a wedge between the Blackthorns and their elven allies.
