## Intro

The dust settles on docks strewn with shattered crates and torn sails. The air smells of ozone and wet wood. Dockworkers creep from hiding, whispering in fear.

{actor: astrid-blackthorn} The woman ranger sheathes her blade and approaches. Up close, she is plainly noble despite the grime of battle. She wipes drake blood from her cheek and gives a stiff, respectful nod.

{speaker: astrid-blackthorn, delivery: her poise returning} I am Lady Astrid Blackthorn. It seems I am in your debt. Few in this city would run toward a drake while everyone else ran away. Well met.

{actor: petra-velan} Before the adventurers can answer, a dockworker named Petra calls from the ship’s deck.

{speaker: petra-velan, to: astrid-blackthorn, delivery: shouting} Astrid! Look at the cage! It wasn’t the beast! This was sabotage!

{actor: astrid-blackthorn} Astrid climbs aboard to inspect the cage’s melted steel, her face darkening.

{speaker: astrid-blackthorn, delivery: muttering} Acid. Someone wanted this creature loose. Someone wanted chaos for my family.

{actor: astrid-blackthorn} She turns to the adventurers.

{speaker: astrid-blackthorn} There is more at play here than simple theft.

## GM Notes

Astrid is impressed and sees the players as useful tools or allies. She will ask what they were doing at the docks. 

**Information to share:**
- If they mention the missing shipment, she confirms it was a Blackthorn delivery of "cultural artifacts." Where the pieces came from and who bought them is her brother Thaddeus's business, she says, and she says no more of it.
- If they mention Silverhand, her jaw tightens. "The Raskovs are scavengers, but they usually aren't this bold."
- She does not know Joran Antonov and has never heard his name.
- She suspects a third party is trying to drive a wedge between the Blackthorns and their elven allies.
- No one saw who poured the acid. The elven crew spent the night ashore at the festival and left the hold barred. The cage key never left the captain's belt. The saboteur is not found here. The acid was plain alchemist's acid. Detect Magic finds nothing on the cage, and no spell or check traces who poured it.
- If the heroes carried the lockbox out of Warehouse 14 unopened, it opens with the key left in its lock, no roll. Inside are the manifest and the map from Warehouse 14's notes. Astrid sees the manifest and asks for it as Blackthorn property. She does not take it by force.
- Asked whether she is the Lady that Warehouse 14's guards waited for, she says Blackthorn cargo is held for whoever the house sends, neither admits nor denies it, and turns the talk back to the saboteur.
- Asked who knew the drake was aboard, she says half the quay watched the cage loaded, and names no one.
- If the heroes offer to hunt the saboteur, she always accepts, whatever else was said, and points them to the whispers of a Covenant in the Lower Districts.

**Pace.** Two rounds. In the second round, or sooner if the heroes start to leave, Astrid draws out her invitation. She does not draw it in the first round. Close the scene then. A hero who refuses Astrid or the invitation here is answered in the next scene.

## Transitions

- To [[encounter:an-invitation]] when Astrid decides to bring the adventurers into her inner circle, the heroes start to leave the docks, or the second round ends.
