## Teaser

The March of Davos is a wild and rugged frontier in the realm of Myr, where the ancient traditions of its native Valkaran population clash with the ambitions of the Asterian Empire. This setting is rich with political intrigue, forgotten magic, and deep-seated tensions, particularly within its bustling capital, Kordavos. Adventurers are drawn to this land of opportunity, where fame, fortune, and hidden knowledge await those brave enough to seek them.

## Summary

The March of Davos, a wild frontier in the Realm of Myr, is a land of stark contrasts where the ancient ways of its native Valkaran people are constantly challenged by the expansionist Asterian Empire. Kordavos, the capital, serves as a vital trade hub and a nexus of underlying tensions, political intrigue, and forgotten magic. Adventurers are drawn into this dynamic setting, often arriving in Kordavos during the annual Valkaran Harvest Festival. Their journey quickly leads them into the heart of the city's conflicts, whether by investigating a missing noble shipment containing ancient Valkaran relics for the Blackthorn family or by seeking a vanished individual like Joran Antonov, who was pursuing dangerous truths about his father's mysterious death.

As the adventurers delve deeper, they become entangled with Kordavos's three powerful rival clans: the Blackthorns, who have aligned with Asterian customs to amass wealth and power; the opportunistic Raskov Clan, known for their smuggling operations; and the traditionalist Valtor Clan, deeply rooted in Valkaran heritage and the Church of Valkara. The investigation reveals that a mysterious organization, the Covenant of the Cowl, is at play. Initially suspected of being connected to the Raskovs, the Covenant is, in fact, the clandestine wing of the Valtor Clan, secretly working to undermine Asterian influence and restore Valkaran dominance, even orchestrating events like the sabotage of noble shipments.

The narrative escalates during a lavish Blackthorn Masquerade Ball, where political machinations come to a head. A shocking assassination occurs, directly linked to the Covenant and revealing their secret network of underground tunnels beneath the city. The pursuit into these catacombs leads to a climactic confrontation where Joran Antonov, previously a missing person, is dramatically revealed as the Champion of the Covenant. He accuses Astrid Blackthorn of his father's murder and reveals his possession of the "Key of Ilmarin," an artifact with the power to magically secure or unlock any opening. At this critical juncture, the adventurers face a pivotal choice: expose the Valtor Clan's hidden operations, bring down the Covenant, or align with their radical agenda to preserve Valkaran traditions against the powerful Blackthorn family. This decision will determine the future of Kordavos and the delicate balance of power within the March of Davos.

## Aftermath: Potential Outcomes

Based on the choices made during the Final Confrontation, the story of the March of Davos resolves in one of the following ways:

### 1. The Valkaran Restoration (Siding with Joran/Covenant)
- **Scene: The New Dawn**: The city of Kordavos wakes to find the Asterian banners replaced by the ancient symbols of Valkara. Joran stands at the balcony of the Blackthorn mansion, declaring the city's independence while Seraphina Valtor prepares for the political transition.
- **Encounter: Securing the Gates**: The party must assist the Covenant in preventing a desperate escape by Asterian loyalists and Blackthorn mercenaries who are attempting to make off with the city's remaining treasury and several sensitive documents.

### 2. The Imperial Grip (Siding with Astrid/Blackthorns)
- **Scene: Vengeance of the Blackthorn**: A somber, high-security state funeral for Thaddeus is held in the grand square. Lady Astrid, now officially the head of the house, makes a hard-line proclamation against 'insurgents' and 'the old ways,' tightening the Blackthorns' control over the March.
- **Encounter: Rooting Out the Nest**: Astrid tasks the party with a surgical strike on a secret Valtor sanctuary to capture Seraphina and retrieve any remaining stolen artifacts, effectively ending the Covenant's visible influence.

### 3. The Wayfinder’s Truce (Neutral/Church Intervention)
- **Scene: The Dust Settles**: If the party chooses to neutralize the Key or hand it to the Church, Sister Danica and Bishop Nicolai (assuming his secret isn't out) broker a tense ceasefire. Kordavos enters a period of cold peace, with neither the Blackthorns nor the Valtors holding absolute sway.
- **Encounter: The Silent Vigil**: During the peace negotiations at the Old Grand Library, extremists from both the Raskov and Valtor clans attempt a coordinated sabotage to restart the war. The party must act as neutral peacekeepers to prevent the city from descending into total anarchy.

## Conclusion

The conclusion of the March of Davos marks a shifting tide in Kordavos. The resolution of the conflict over the "Key of Ilmarin" reshapes the power balance between the rival clans and determines the level of Asterian influence in the region. Regardless of the choice, the Key remains a central artifact of power, its true potential yet to be fully realized as the different factions retreat to plan their next moves.

## Sequel Hooks: The Covenant of the Cowl

The fallout of the confrontation leads directly into the next chapter of the saga:

- **The Resistance**: For parties that sided with the Blackthorns, they must now root out the remaining cells of the Covenant across the March and confront Seraphina Valtor directly.
- **The Ascendance**: For parties that joined Joran, they are tasked with securing the artifacts mentioned in his father's journal to cement the Covenant's power and prepare for the total expulsion of Asterian influence.
- **The Key's Secret**: If the Key was activated, its resonance has awakened something deeper in the Valkarr Forest—a gateway to an ancient Valkaran stronghold that both factions crave.
