## Summary

Ilya is a graceful half-elf with laughing grey eyes, dark hair worn loose to his collar, and a festival coat of Valkaran embroidery over Asterian cut, a diplomatic garment if ever there was one. A seven-stringed gusle rides across his back, and his smile opens doors that coin cannot.

Born to a Valkaran mother and an elven trader father, Ilya grew up belonging to every hall in Kordavos and none of them. He made himself indispensable instead: the skald who plays the Blackthorn salons, the Raskov taverns, and the Valtor feast-halls alike, trading songs for secrets in each. Lately the secrets have grown darker, whispers of a cowled brotherhood and of great houses buying old relics, and Ilya suspects the next verse of Kordavos's song will be written in blood unless someone changes the tune.

Charming, observant, and incorrigibly curious, Ilya wants to shape how the story of Kordavos ends, and to be the one who sings it afterward. The city raised him when no clan would claim him, and he will not watch it tear itself apart.
