## Summary

Branka is a broad-shouldered dwarf with a weathered face, a nose broken at least twice, and copper hair worked into tight braids. She wears a battered breastplate stamped with the old gate-ward sigil of Kordavos and carries a round steel shield scarred by years of honest use.

The Stoneveils guarded the gates of Kordavos for four generations under the old Valkaran marcher lords. When the Asterian Empire restructured the city watch, Branka's family lost their post and their standing overnight. She has worked as a shield-for-hire ever since, escorting caravans through the March, and has returned to Kordavos for the Harvest Festival, where the sight of the gates her family once kept stirs old pride and old grievances alike.

Blunt, steady, and utterly dependable, Branka wants to restore her family's honor by proving that a Stoneveil shield still means something in Kordavos, and she will not stand by while schemers, whether Asterian or Valkaran, endanger the ordinary folk of the city.
