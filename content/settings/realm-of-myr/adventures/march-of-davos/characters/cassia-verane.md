## Summary

Cassia is a precise Asterian woman with ink-stained fingers, dark hair coiled in the capital fashion, and a grey travelling coat lined with map pockets. She carries a brass surveyor's glass and a thick folio of hand-drawn charts of the tunnels and ruins beneath Kordavos.

Trained as a cartographer-mage in the Asterian capital, Cassia was posted to Kordavos to chart the March for the imperial survey. The work led her beneath the city, where her charts began to show what her superiors did not want recorded: old Valkaran catacombs, sealed doors bearing wards she could not break, and fresh boot prints in passages that should have been empty for a century. Her latest report was returned unread, so she has resolved to find the truth herself.

Methodical, dryly witty, and loyal to knowledge over empire, Cassia wants to map the hidden passages beneath Kordavos and understand the Valkaran wardings sealed within them before someone with worse intentions gets there first.
