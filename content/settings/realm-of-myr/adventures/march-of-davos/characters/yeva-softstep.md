## Summary

Yeva is a small, quick halfling with sharp hazel eyes, a mess of dark curls pinned under a festival cap, and clothes cut for blending into crowds. Half a dozen hidden pockets hold picks, wires, and at least one item she swears she was only holding for a friend.

Yeva grew up picking pockets during the Harvest Festival in the Lower Districts of Kordavos, and graduated to what she calls freelance acquisitions. She knows the black market stalls, the smugglers' shortcuts, and which Raskov warehouses are never really empty. A job went sour last season when a crate she lifted turned out to hold an old Valkaran relic someone very dangerous wanted back, and she has been watching the shadows ever since.

Quick-witted, irreverent, and disarmingly cheerful, Yeva wants to find out who was moving that relic and why it nearly cost her her life. She also would not mind a payday large enough to finally buy her own tavern in the Lower Districts.
