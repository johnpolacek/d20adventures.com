## Summary

Milos is a lean Valkaran man in his forties with a close-cropped grey beard, road-worn boots, and the undyed travelling robes of a Wayfinder priest. A bronze compass-star of the Church of Valkara hangs at his chest, and his walking staff is notched with the miles of a hundred pilgrimages.

Milos has walked the pilgrim roads of the March of Davos for twenty years as a Wayfinder, a wandering priest who tends shrines and travellers alike. Of late he has heard troubling whispers in the villages: shrines stripped of old relics, and a name spoken only in doubt, the Covenant of the Cowl. He has come to Kordavos for the Harvest Festival to seek answers from the Church, and he fears what he may find.

Calm, warm, and quietly observant, Milos wants to protect the soul of Valkaran tradition without letting it curdle into fanaticism. He believes an honest accounting, however painful, is the only way to keep faith and city from tearing each other apart.
