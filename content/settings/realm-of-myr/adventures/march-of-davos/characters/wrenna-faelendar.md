## Summary

Wrenna is a tall, watchful elf with moss-green eyes, ash-blonde hair bound in a scout's knot, and travel leathers dyed the grey-green of the Valkarr Forest. A longbow of pale yew rides at her shoulder, and she moves through city streets as quietly as forest paths.

Wrenna is a scout of the reclusive elven settlement of Faelendar, deep in the forests of the March. The elders sent her to Kordavos when word reached them that old Valkaran relics, things the elves helped seal away long ago, had begun to surface in the city's markets and noble houses. She arrived with the festival crowds, posing as a fur trader, and what she has seen so far troubles her.

Reserved, patient, and dry-humored, Wrenna must learn who is unearthing the old relics and stop them before something the elves buried for good reason is brought back into the light. She also quietly hopes to prove to her elders that the peoples of Kordavos are worth trusting, not just watching.
