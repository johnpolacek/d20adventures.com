## Intro

The next morning Thalbern is home in the forest. Wollandora's words stay with him: stolen relics, a suspicious shipment, Kordavos.

{actor: thalbern, place: gear, object: pack} He packs plain clothes and wraps his longbow and quiver out of sight, with his sword and dagger.

He does not take the road. Wollandora saw the boat on the river, Valkaran-made and riding low. Thalbern follows the Mordava downstream.
