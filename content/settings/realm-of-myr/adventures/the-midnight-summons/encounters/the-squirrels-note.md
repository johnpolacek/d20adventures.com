## Intro

Thalbern is a ranger of the Valkarr woods, raised by the forest's elves. He lives alone at the edge of the trees and keeps clear of Kordavos and its politics.

{actor: thalbern} Late in the afternoon he is mending a bowstring by his cottage.

{place: door} Something scratches at the cottage door. A red squirrel sits on the step with a rolled note in its teeth.

It chatters at him and will not come any closer.

## GM Notes

This is the first scene of the game, and it teaches a new player how to play while the story starts. Keep every reply short and plain. Do not explain the rules or the screen. End each reply by naming one or two things Thalbern could do next, in the story's own words, so a new player sees what they might type.

Thalbern wants the note. He can coax the squirrel, which is an easy Animal Handling check, DC 10. On a success it hops to him and lets him take the note. On a failure it drops the note on the step and darts up the oak, scolding him. Either way he gets the note.

He can cast Speak with Animals instead. The squirrel says a druid who smells of moss sent it, that she looked worried, and that it was promised acorns.

The note is in the hand of Wollandora, an elven druid and his trusted friend: Meet me at the Old Standing Stones at midnight. The balance of the forest could depend on it.

Once he has the note, he has time before he must leave. He can ready his gear, look over his bow, sword and dagger, or rest. Nothing here harms him.

If the player sets out for the stones before reading the note, he takes the note with him and reads it on the way. A player who knows the game can leave at once.
