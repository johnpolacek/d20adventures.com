## Intro

Eyes shine in the shadows of the tree line, where the sound came from. A hulking monster crashes out of the undergrowth, fifteen feet tall, with the body of a giant bear and the head of an owl. It lets out a guttural squawk, agitated and territorial.

## GM Notes

The Owlbear attacks. A high difficulty Animal Handling check lets Thalbern move past it. If he wins initiative and hides, a medium difficulty Stealth check lets him move past it. If he is badly wounded, below 25% health, Wollandora appears and drives off the Owlbear: transition to 'timely-rescue'. If he defeats the Owlbear, describe his victory in a sentence or two and transition to 'meeting-at-the-stones'.

## Transitions

- To [[encounter:meeting-at-the-stones]] when Thalbern defeats the Owlbear, manages to evade it, successfully uses Animal Handling to pacify and move past it, or successfully rolls any other way to move past it.
- To [[encounter:timely-rescue]] when Thalbern is badly wounded by the Owlbear, below 25% health.
