## Intro

{actor: wolf, from: pathOut, pace: sneak} The squirrel shrieks and bolts up the oak. A grey wolf slinks out of the trees, thin and hungry.

{actor: wolf, target: thalbern} It sees Thalbern standing over his gear, lowers its head and snarls.

## GM Notes

This is the game's first fight, a small one, and it teaches a new player how a fight goes. Keep every reply short and plain. Do not explain the rules or the screen. End each reply by naming one or two things Thalbern could do next, such as shooting his bow, drawing his sword, or shouting it off.

The wolf is alone and hungry. Thalbern can fight it. He can scare it off with a medium Intimidation check, DC 13, or calm it with a medium Animal Handling check, DC 13, and it slinks away. Speak with Animals lets him talk to it: it is starving, and a scrap of food sends it off.

The wolf fights on until it is down to 3 hit points or fewer, so the player sees it take its turns and bite. Then it runs into the woods. A blow that takes it to 0 hit points kills it. Say in a sentence how it goes.

The Old Standing Stones are deeper in the Valkarr woods, not toward Kordavos. When Thalbern leaves, he heads into the woods for the stones.

If Thalbern falls to 0 hit points, the wolf drags off a sack of his supplies and is gone. He comes to a little later, sore, and sets out for the stones.

## Transitions

- To [[encounter:broken-silence]] when the wolf is dead, has run off, or has been scared or calmed away, or when Thalbern falls to 0 hit points and the wolf leaves with his supplies.
