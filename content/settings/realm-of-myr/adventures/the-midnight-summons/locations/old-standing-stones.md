## Summary

A ring of ancient monoliths standing in a clearing deep within the Valkarr Forest. Moonlight pools in the clearing when the canopy opens above the circle, and the site carries the weight of the old ways — it is where Wollandora summons those she trusts when the balance of the forest is threatened.
