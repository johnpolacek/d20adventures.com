## Summary

The Valkarr Forest is an ancient wood bordering the human city of Kordavos. Its elves have kept the old ways — and the Valkaran relics — safe for centuries, maintaining the balance of the forest. The canopy is dense enough that almost no moonlight reaches the forest floor at night, and its deep paths are known only to rangers and druids. Territorial beasts, including owlbears, roam its darker reaches.
