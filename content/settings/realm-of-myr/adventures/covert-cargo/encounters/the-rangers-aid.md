## Intro

The pier is quiet again. Mist drifts over the water, and the lanterns swing a little on their hooks above the crate and the dead man beside it.

Boots sound on the gangway and cross the foredeck. The stranger from the treeline ducks through the saloon door with his bow in one hand. He is lean and weathered, with long, tangled brown hair, and his sharp green eyes pass over the crate and the body before they settle on the wounded.

"You'll live. Sit, and let me see to that," the ranger says. He kneels, already reaching for the pouch of herbs at his belt.

"I am Thalbern. I followed this boat down the river from the Valkarr woods." He glances back through the door at the dark bank. "You as well, little one. Come out of the leaves. And then I think you had better tell me what this boat was carrying."

## GM Notes

Reinhard lies dead in the saloon with Thalbern's arrow in him. Silas is gone. He has gone for more Blackthorn men and will come back for the crate within hours. If the players brought Silas down themselves, he lies where he fell and it is his masters who will send men.

Thalbern dresses the wounds with herbs and clean linen. Each wounded player character recovers to about three quarters health.

He says little about himself. If asked, a druid of the Valkarr woods sent him to find Valkaran relics taken from the elven vaults, and he tracked this boat downriver. He wants to know what the boat carried and who was buying. He will not open or take the crate himself, and he does not join the party.

He advises them to be gone before the Blackthorn men return. Kordavos is three miles through the forest, and the Harvest Festival crowds will hide them. He allows that they may meet again in the city. When the players turn to the crate or leave, he slips out to watch the river trail.

Continue this encounter until the players decide what to do next.
