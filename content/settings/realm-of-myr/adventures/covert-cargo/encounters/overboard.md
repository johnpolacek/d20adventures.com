## Intro

{actor: 1749159962941} The river closes over Lyra, black and shockingly cold. For a moment there is no up or down, only her coat dragging at her and water roaring in her ears. Then her head breaks the surface and she drags in a breath.

Above her the tug is already moving. A blade flashes at the bow as the last mooring line parts, and the current carries the boat out into the channel, lamps glowing, the crate aboard, no one at the rail looking back. Then the mist has it, and its light fades downstream toward Kordavos.

{actor: 1749159962941} The bank is close. Lyra's hands find reeds, then mud. She hauls herself out beside the empty gangway, coughing up river water, and stands shivering in her soaked coat.

{place: pierEnd} The pier is quiet again. The lantern at the gangway's end swings over open water where the boat lay.

## GM Notes

The tug is gone downstream with the crate and whoever of Silas and Reinhard still stands, bound for Kordavos and their Blackthorn masters. It does not come back, and it cannot be caught on foot or by swimming.

Lyra is out of the water and in no danger of drowning. She is soaked, cold and bruised. If her hands were bound she needs help or a sharp edge to free them.

Poppen and Thalbern each saw her go into the river, each from his own hiding place. A watcher who comes out to help her is plainly seen by her and by the other watcher, with no check needed. This is most likely where the three first meet. They are strangers, with no names until they give them, and each knows only what he saw from where he was. A watcher who stays hidden stays unknown: anyone looking for him needs a Perception check, DC 15.

Thalbern carries herbs and clean linen. If he tends a wounded hero, that hero recovers to about three quarters health.

Lyra knows the crate holds something powerfully enchanted, that the two men meant to silence her, and that the boat was bound for Kordavos. Thalbern knows a boat carrying relics from the elven vaults has just gone downriver. Poppen knows only what he saw and overheard.

Kordavos is three miles through the forest by the bank trail, and the Harvest Festival crowds will hide them. Continue this encounter until the heroes decide what to do next.
