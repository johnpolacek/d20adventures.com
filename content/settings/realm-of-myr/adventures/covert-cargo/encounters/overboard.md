## GM Notes

The tug is gone downstream and out of sight in the mist with the crate and whoever of Silas and Reinhard still stands, bound for Kordavos and their Blackthorn masters. It does not come back, and it cannot be caught on foot or by swimming.

Lyra is out of the water and in no danger of drowning. She is soaked, cold and bruised. If her hands were bound she needs help or a sharp edge to free them.

Poppen and Thalbern each saw her go into the river, each from his own hiding place. A watcher who comes out to help her is plainly seen by her and by the other watcher, with no check needed. The mist lies on the river, not the bank: those who stand together on the pier see each other plainly. Lyra is out of the water when the scene opens and stays out unless she goes back in. This is most likely where the three first meet. They are strangers, with no names until they give them, and each knows only what he saw from where he was. A watcher who stays hidden stays unknown: anyone looking for him needs a Perception check, DC 15.

Thalbern carries herbs and clean linen. Dressing a wound restores no hit points, as SRD 5.2.1 has it: a Wisdom (Medicine) check, DC 10, stabilizes a hero at 0 hit points, and a failed one leaves the hero dying. Hit points come back only by magic, as Thalbern's Cure Wounds, or by a rest.

Lyra knows the crate holds something powerfully enchanted, that the two men meant to silence her, and that the boat was bound for Kordavos. Thalbern knows a boat carrying relics from the elven vaults of the Valkarr woods has just gone downriver, and that the druid Wollandora sent him after them. Poppen knows only what he saw and overheard.

Kordavos is three miles through the forest by the bank trail, and the Harvest Festival crowds will hide them. Continue this encounter until the heroes decide what to do next.
