## Summary

Docked on a discrete pier on a quiet fork of the Mordava River, a river boat bearing a secret shipment bound for Kordavos sets a clandestine plot in motion.

## Premise

Lyra and Poppen have never met and are not working together. Lyra came aboard with Silas and Reinhard, who hired her. She does not know Poppen, his name, or that he is there.

Poppen is alone and is here by chance. He is spying on the pier in the hope of a score. He knows nobody here, by name or by sight, and knows nothing of the deal, the crate or anyone's orders beyond what he sees and overhears.

Silas, Reinhard and the elves do not know Poppen or that anyone is watching. If he is noticed he is a stranger to them, an unknown voice or a small figure with no name.
