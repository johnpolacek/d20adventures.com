## Intro

The Masquerade Ball fills the Blackthorn Mansion with marble, gold and candlelight. Nobles and merchants in masks drift between tables of rare wine and delicacies.

It is a world away from the hardship of the Valkarans outside. Beneath the glitter, intrigue moves among the guests.

## GM Notes

If the characters listen or pass a perception check, they can hear whispers among some guests hint at disapproval of such extravagance, especially during times when many Valkarans struggle. Others admire the Blackthorn’s forward thinking.

From behind elaborate masks, a few guests murmur in hushed tones, expressing quiet disapproval of the Blackthorns’ extravagance. "All this, while many in the city can barely afford bread," one voice whispers, her tone sharp with judgment. Another adds, "They’ve forgotten their roots... forsaking the people for Asterian opulence."

Yet, not all share this sentiment. Other guests admire the Blackthorns' ambition and speak of their "forward-thinking" ways. "They know how to seize opportunity," says a man in a richly embroidered cloak. "In times like these, it’s visionaries like him who will secure the future of Kordavos."

**One round of mingling.** The party has one round to take in the hall, try the wine and listen to the guests. At the end of that round, a woman in a golden mask notices them and makes her way over through the crowd. That is Lady Astrid, and the next scene opens as she reaches them. Take the transition then, whatever the heroes are doing. The guests here are unnamed and speak no more than the lines above.

**No one leaves the ballroom yet.** Servants and house guards keep guests out of the kitchens, the service doors and the halls beyond. A guest who tries is turned back with a bow and steered toward the wine, with no roll and no alarm. The hall holds no magic beyond the candles and the jewels on display.
