## Intro

Dawn breaks over Kordavos to the sound of bells. The Asterian banners are gone from the gate towers, and the old symbols of Valkara hang in their place.

From the Blackthorn balcony, Joran Antonov raises the Key of Ilmarin and declares the city free. Seraphina Valtor stands at his shoulder.

Not everyone cheers. Blackthorn mercenaries and Asterian loyalists have barred themselves in the counting houses by the river, and mean to flee with the city's treasury.

The March of Davos has chosen its old ways. Whether it can hold them is a story for another day.

## GM Notes

The adventurers sided with Joran and the Covenant of the Cowl. This is the end of Welcome to Kordavos: the Valkaran Restoration.

If every hero died in the inner sanctum, they are not here. Joran raises the Key alone, and the heroes are mourned among the fallen.
