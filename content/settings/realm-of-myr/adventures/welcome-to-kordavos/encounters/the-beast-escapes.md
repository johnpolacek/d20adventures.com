## Intro

The adventurers rush from the warehouse. The elven ship *Star of Faelendar* rocks in its berth, and a roar rises from its hold.

The hold doors burst outward. A massive **Forest Drake** emerges, mossy green and bark-brown, its yellow eyes burning.

{actor: astrid-blackthorn, object: blade} At the gangplank, a tall woman in leather armor draws a curved blade.

{speaker: astrid-blackthorn, delivery: shouting to the fleeing crew} Keep it contained! If that beast reaches the Lower Districts, it’ll be a massacre!

{actor: astrid-blackthorn} She spots the adventurers and waves them over.

{speaker: astrid-blackthorn} You! If you can fight, help me!

## GM Notes

This is a high-action combat encounter. The Forest Drake is confused and aggressive, attacking the nearest targets.

### Containing the Beast

- **The Forest Drake:** Breathes its *Poison Breath* when it is ready and two or more of its foes stand before it. Otherwise it attacks with claw and jaw, its *Rend*. It may attempt to fly up to the roofs of warehouses if pressured.
- **Astrid Blackthorn:** Fights alongside the players. She uses her bow to pin the creature's wings or distract it from vulnerable dockworkers.
- **Objective:** The players don't necessarily need to kill the drake; they can use chains, magic, or heavy nets found on the docks to restrain it. Astrid will prioritize capture over killing if possible.

**Checks.** The drake fights by its stat block. Every blow against it is an attack roll, and every net and spell lands only on a roll.
- Bloodied, at half its hit points or fewer, it is bleeding and slowed, and looks to the roofs. At 0 hit points it is dead.
- Heavy cargo nets lie by the crane. Throwing one over it is Athletics DC 15 and leaves it Restrained. Pinning a netted drake is Athletics DC 15. Two nets on it, or a net pinned, hold it.
- Chains or ropes hold it on the check's success. A spell that leaves it Restrained or Incapacitated, such as Entangle or Sleep, holds it while the spell lasts.
- A spell or trick that leaves it Prone or Blinded, such as Grease, lets the next net land with no roll.
- Dockworkers caught on the quay run when a hero tells them to, no roll. Astrid shields the rest.
- If it takes to a roof, a hero reaches it by the crane or a stack of crates, Athletics DC 10. Arrows and spells reach it there. A net thrown up from the quay at a drake on a roof is Athletics DC 15.
- The hold of the *Star of Faelendar* holds the broken cage, straw and crates of elven silk and wine, and nothing worth stealing.

**Pace.** Three rounds at most. Astrid's arrows and nets count like the heroes'. If the drake is not held or beaten down by the end of the third round, it tears free, beats up over the roofs and flies north toward the Valkarr Forest. That counts as driven off.

**Astrid mid-fight.** Asked anything that is not about the drake, she says "Questions after." and answers nothing until the next scene.

### The Reveal

During the fight, characters with a **DC 15 Perception** notice the Drake's cage on the ship was not broken from the inside—the hinges look melted or corroded, suggesting intentional sabotage. The drake carries no enchantment or compulsion, and its chains are plain harbor iron.
