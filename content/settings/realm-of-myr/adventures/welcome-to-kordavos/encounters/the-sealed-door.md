## Intro

{actor: eamon, effect: burst, element: radiant} Eamon casts a flare of light, and the seamless wall reveals a massive door of black iron, its sigils pulsing like a heartbeat. The air is freezing.

{actor: astrid-blackthorn, object: blade} Astrid grips her sword.

{speaker: astrid-blackthorn} This is the inner sanctum. Whatever they’ve been hiding, and whoever killed my brother, is through that door.

{actor: eamon} Eamon steps forward, sweating despite the cold.

{speaker: eamon, delivery: strained} This seal is different. It’s tied to the city’s own ley lines. If I break it, I’m ringing a bell for everyone inside. Are you ready?

## GM Notes

**Breaking the Seal (Skill Challenge):**
Instead of Eamon doing it alone, have the party assist him. 
- **Arcana**: To stabilize the feedback.
- **Athletics**: To physically brace the shifting stone frame.
- **Persuasion/Intimidation**: To keep Eamon focused as the psychic weight of the door pushes back.

The doorway holds no trap.

Each check is DC 15. One check per hero per round. Eamon makes no check of his own.

Requires 3 successes before 2 failures. On a failure, the door opens but the party is *Surprised* in the next room by the first wave of undead.

## Transitions

- To [[encounter:big-empty]] once the seal is broken.
