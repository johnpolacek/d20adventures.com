## Intro

{place: gateArch} The adventurers approach the grand gates of Kordavos, hung with Asterian and Valkaran banners. Music from the Harvest Festival carries from beyond.

{place: ledger} First they must wait in a long line of travelers that snakes back from a table beneath the arch. It has barely moved in an hour.

{place: sergeant} The sergeant stands and bellows down the line: "Festival law! No one passes this gate alone. Travelers enter as a company, every name written in the gate book together, and each of you answers for the others while you're in the city."

{place: lineBack} Families and merchant trains close ranks. Those who came alone are left standing near one another, a few places back from the table.

{hero: branka-stoneveil} A broad dwarf with copper braids stares up at the old gate-ward sigil over the arch. The same sigil is stamped on her battered breastplate.

{hero: milos-radan} A grey-bearded priest in undyed robes, the bronze compass-star of the Church of Valkara at his chest, shares water with a weary pilgrim family.

{hero: thalbern} A lean, weathered man in a cloak of forest hues stands apart, longbow on his back, river mud on his boots.

{hero: 1749159962941} A young scholar in spectacles keeps her hood up, peeling apart the river-soaked pages of a book.

{hero: 1749307435667} A halfling in a patchwork waistcoat works the line, trading a joke for every rumor.

{place: ledger} Up at the table, a guard turns back a family three marks short of the fee. Wagons in Blackthorn colors roll past the whole line, waved through without a glance. A peddler who walks up to the table is marched back to the end of the line. Behind the strangers, a spice trader grumbles that he has stood here since dawn.

The line will not move for a while. The only company is each other.

## GM Notes

This scene is where the heroes meet, before anyone at the gate asks them anything. It lasts one round. Each hero takes one turn in the line: to size up the others, strike up talk, ask about something they see, or offer to stand together. Then the line moves up to the sergeant's table. Give the heroes room to talk to each other. No one in this scene is in charge of them.

**Festival law.** Last year's Festival ended in a brawl between the clans, so the governor revived an old Valkaran custom of surety. During the Festival no traveler enters alone. Travelers enter as a company, their names written together in the gate book, and each answers to the watch for what the others do in the city. Families and merchant trains already count as companies. The heroes each came alone, so they must choose each other or wait outside until the Festival ends. The Stoneveils kept this custom when they were the gate-wards, and Branka knows it as her great-grandfather's law.

**The table is out of reach.** The sergeant at the table is Garlan Ironfist. He is too far up the line to hear the heroes or be heard, and calls no one up in this scene. Guards keep the line in order. Anyone who leaves their place to walk up to the table, or tries to slip past the arch, is marched back to the end of the line, as the peddler in the intro was. A hero who tries it is turned back the same way, in a sentence, and loses nothing else. Other heroes do not follow them up. They keep their places, so the one turned back rejoins them. A hero's business with the gate waits for the table.

**Between the heroes.** Each hero shows something the others can notice and ask about: Branka's sigil, Milos's compass-star, Thalbern's river mud, Lyra's soaked books, Poppen's questions. Any other hero shows the detail their own paragraph in the intro gives. When one hero asks another about it, leave the answer to that hero. Never answer for them. A hero with no paragraph in the intro is shown the first time the story turns to them, with one detail of how they look.

**Things in the line to talk about.**
- The family turned back three marks short. A hero may pay for them, and the family thanks them as it passes.
- The Blackthorn wagons waved through without inspection, while everyone else waits.
- The spice trader behind them, who has stood here since dawn and has opinions about the watch.
- A guard pulling a nervous traveler out of the line to search his pack.

The people in the line have no character sheets. Tell what they do and say in narration.

**Names.** No hero knows another's name. A hero may give theirs, ask for another's, or keep it. Whatever is said aloud is heard by all the heroes, who stand together.

**A hero who keeps to themselves.** Describe what the others can see of them. Do not force them to speak. Another hero may come to them.

**A hero who wants to go alone.** They may stay in the line. The law will catch up with them at the table.

## Transitions

- To [[encounter:the-front-of-the-line]] when the first round ends. The line moves up and every hero reaches the sergeant's table together, whether or not they have agreed to stand together.
