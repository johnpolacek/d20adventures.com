## Teaser

Travellers from across the realm head to the March of Davos seeking their fortune in a land of bustling cities and untamed wildlands. Who will you run into on the road to Kordavos?

## Summary

Travellers from across the Realm of Myr travel to the March of Davos, seeking their fortune in this land of bustling cities and untamed wildlands. On this occasion, many of them head to the capital and its annual Harvest Festival. Rumors of clan rivalries, ancient relics of a lost civilization and opportunities for those who seek riches and power have drawn much interest from far and wide. Who will you run into on the road to Kordavos.

## Premise

This is a prelude. The heroes meet on the Marquis Road, the last day's walk to Kordavos, and hear something of the city before they reach its gates. There is no fight on the road and nothing here is meant to harm them. Its people are good company and know only what any traveller hears.

The heroes travel alone, each on their own business, and have never met. They do not know each other's names, errands or pasts, only what they can see of each other on the road. That changes only as the story shows them talking, introducing themselves or hearing a name said aloud.

Heroes who come on together from the adventure just before this one, as the story so far tells, already know each other and walk the road together. They come up from the river the same morning, three miles from the gates, with the fight at the pier only hours behind them. They know nothing of the other heroes, who know nothing of them.

Thalbern, Lyra and Poppen, played without the story so far, each come alone, as their own pasts say. Each knows only what they saw at the smugglers' pier on the Mordava. None of them knows the other two were there.

Dmitri, Elaraen, Sister Mira and Borina know none of the heroes.

## Hero Hooks

- Bringing an offering to the Church of Valkara for the Harvest rites.
- Owed coin by Silverhand, a smuggler of the Lower Districts, and going to collect it.
- Taking up a post in the Asterian governor's service after the Festival.
- Coming home to the March for the first Festival in years.
- Chasing word of old Valkaran relics changing hands in the city.
- Looking for guard work, since the Festival's merchants pay well.
- Carrying a sealed letter for Sister Danica of the Old Grand Library, and told not to read it.
- Hired to guard a merchant's wagon to the Festival, and paid off at the last inn on the road.
- Passing through on the road north, and stopping for the Festival.
