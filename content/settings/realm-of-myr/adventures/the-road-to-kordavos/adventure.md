## Summary

Travellers from across the Realm of Myr travel to the March of Davos, seeking their fortune in this land of bustling cities and untamed wildlands. On this occasion, many of them head to the capital and its annual Harvest Festival. Rumors of clan rivalries, ancient relics of a lost civilization and opportunities for those who seek riches and power have drawn much interest from far and wide. Who will you run into on the road to Kordavos.
