How the narrator says this setting's names. A name not listed here is guessed from its spelling, and the guess can drift from one sentence to the next. A listed one is said the same way every time, in every voice. Not part of the wiki graph: each adventure package carries this list (scripts/adventure-packages.ts), and the desktop narrator reads it.

One name to a line. A sound-alike spelling in quotes, or the sounds themselves between slashes, British then American when they differ. `pnpm --filter @d20/desktop narrator:names` lists every made-up name with how it is said now, and `--say <dir> <names>` renders each in every voice to listen to.
