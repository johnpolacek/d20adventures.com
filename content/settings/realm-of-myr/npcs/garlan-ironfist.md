## Summary

A burly man with a stern face, kind eyes, and a well-groomed beard. He wears the polished armor of the Kordavos city guard, with a blue-and-gold tabard showcasing the city's crest.

Diligent and fair, Garlan takes his duty seriously but can show kindness to those who need it.
