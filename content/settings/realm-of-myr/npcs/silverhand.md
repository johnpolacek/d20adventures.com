## Summary

Silverhand, the alias of Kazimir Raskov, is the head of the Raskov Clan’s smuggling operations in Kordavos. He is a tall, slender man who exudes an air of cold calculation and predatory stillness. He is famously recognized by his intricate gauntlet of polished silver on his left hand.

Cunning, enigmatic, and stealthy, Silverhand is always two steps ahead, calculating his movements precisely to avoid detection and maintain his grip on the city's illicit trade.
