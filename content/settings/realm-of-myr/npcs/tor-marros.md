## Summary

A towering figure in his early forties, Tor is bald and heavily muscled, standing well over six feet tall. His arms are covered in crude tattoos of animals and mythical creatures, and he wears a sleeveless tunic to show them off. His thick neck and powerful build give him the nickname "The Bull." He has a deep, gravelly voice that rumbles when he speaks.

## Behavior

Very strong
