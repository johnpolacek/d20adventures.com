## Summary

Dmitri Volkov is a man of average height with a sturdy build. His white hair and beard is neatly combed, framing a confident, often smiling, face. He dresses in fine, practical clothes, always clean and well-maintained.

Confident, shrewd, affable, and meticulous. He possesses a dry wit and a keen eye for detail. While generally honest in his dealings, he is fiercely protective of his business and can be ruthless when necessary.

Dmitri started his career from humble beginnings, working as a clerk in a trading house. Through shrewd deals, keen observation, and an innate understanding of market trends, he slowly built his own network and capital. He now owns a successful import-export business, specializing in rare spices and exotic textiles, with connections across several major cities.

Dmitri's primary motivation is to expand his mercantile empire and secure his family's legacy. He seeks out new trade routes, rare goods, and influential contacts to further his business. He also harbors a secret desire to be recognized as the most influential merchant in the region, not just for wealth, but for the power and respect it commands.

## Behavior

Dmitri is generally affable and polite, always ready with a charming smile and a firm handshake. However, beneath the pleasant demeanor lies a sharp mind and a shrewd negotiator. He values punctuality and honesty in business, but is not above a bit of calculated manipulation to secure a favorable deal. He can be surprisingly stubborn when his interests are at stake.
