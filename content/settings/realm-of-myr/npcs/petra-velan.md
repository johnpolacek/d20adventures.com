## Summary

A woman in her mid-forties with short, steel-gray hair tied back in a ponytail. Petra walks with a noticeable limp, the result of a ship’s accident years ago, and wears a metal brace on her right leg. She dresses practically in heavy work boots, a leather apron, and a patched shirt, often seen puffing on a pipe. Her tanned skin and muscular build speak to years of hard work.
