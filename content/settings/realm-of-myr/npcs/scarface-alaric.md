## Summary

Tall and lean with a deeply scarred face, jet-black hair tied back in a ponytail, and piercing blue eyes.

Cautious and untrusting, fiercely protective of his crew and territory, has a sharp tongue and quick wit.

## Behavior

Expert in stealth, pickpocketing, and knife fighting.
