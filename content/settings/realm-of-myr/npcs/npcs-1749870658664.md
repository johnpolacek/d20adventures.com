## Summary

Elaraen is a lean half-elf with expressive green eyes that often hold a hint of melancholy. His slightly pointed ears are usually hidden by his long, dark hair, which he often ties back with a simple leather thong. He possesses a charming, easy smile that belies a deeper thoughtfulness. His traveling clothes are practical but well-maintained, often adorned with a colorful scarf or a small, intricate brooch.

Charismatic, witty, and a natural storyteller, Elaraen uses his charm to navigate the world. He possesses a melancholic streak, often masked by his performances and humor. He is fiercely independent and values personal freedom above all else. He is observant and empathetic, often picking up on the subtle cues of others.

Born to an elven mother and a human noble father, Elaraen felt like an outsider in his father's rigid, human-dominated household. His artistic inclinations and free spirit clashed constantly with his father's expectations for him to pursue a more 'respectable' and structured career. After a particularly heated argument about his future, Elaraen, then a young adult, left home to forge his own path. He found solace and purpose in music and storytelling, honing his craft as a traveling performer.

Elaraen is driven by a deep desire to find his own identity and worth through his art, separate from his father's expectations. He seeks to experience the world beyond the confines of his past, to gather stories, and to share his music. Subconsciously, he may also be searching for a sense of belonging and acceptance that he never found at home.

## Behavior

Elaraen is generally affable and quick-witted, using humor and music to connect with people. He is observant and empathetic, often picking up on the moods of those around him. While he enjoys company, he maintains a certain emotional distance, a lingering effect of his estrangement. He can be melancholic when alone, often composing new songs that reflect his inner turmoil or the beauty he finds in the world.
