## Summary

Astrid Blackthorn’s brother and a powerful Blackthorn noble whose ambitions drive much of the Masquerade Ball intrigue. He is head of House Blackthorn. Astrid is his sister, and their parents are long dead.

He is a man, not a beast. The Forest Drake is the caged creature he unveils at his speech.

He admits nothing of the Key of Ilmarin, the missing crate, Mikhail Antonov or his house's past, to anyone and at any time, even dying. Asked, he smiles the question aside or denies it. He never confesses a crime and never names who did one.
