## Summary

A lean grey timber wolf with a darker saddle along its back, a pale throat and pale amber eyes. Thin from a hard season.

Hungry, wary, bold only when cornered or starving.

Find food.

## Behavior

Goes for an easy meal. Fights what stands in its way, and runs once it is hurt badly.
