## Summary

A hulking monstrosity with the body of a bear and the head of an owl. Thick, matted fur, razor-sharp talons, and a powerful beak. Its eyes glow with a feral intensity.

Territorial, aggressive, primal.

Defend its territory, hunt for food.

## Behavior

Attacks perceived threats on sight. Fights with ferocity. Can be driven off by overwhelming force or a display of superior power.
