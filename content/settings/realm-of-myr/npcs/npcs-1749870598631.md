## Summary

Borina is a stout dwarf with a weathered face, framed by long, dark hair braided with silver beads and small, polished stones. Her eyes, a piercing blue, seem to hold ancient secrets. She often wears layers of dark, embroidered robes that rustle with hidden trinkets and pouches.

Wise, eccentric, and deeply insightful, Borina possesses a quiet dignity. She can be cryptic but is fundamentally kind, offering guidance with a gentle, knowing smile. There's a hint of melancholy about her, a lingering sadness from her past, but it's tempered by a profound sense of purpose.

Borina hails from Hammerdeep, a formidable dwarven kingdom renowned for its craftsmanship and rigid traditions. Her innate fascination with divination and the 'strange ways' of foresight were met with disapproval by her kin, who valued tangible craft over ethereal visions. Unable to reconcile her calling with their strict culture, Borina chose exile, leaving her ancestral home to wander the lands, seeking understanding and a place where her unique gifts would be accepted.

Borina is driven by a deep desire to understand the threads of fate and to use her visions to guide others, hoping to prevent misfortune or reveal hidden truths. She also secretly yearns for a place where her unique gifts are not just tolerated, but valued.

## Behavior

Borina is generally calm and observant, often speaking in riddles or metaphors. She has a habit of studying people intently, as if reading their past and future in their expressions. When performing a reading, she becomes deeply focused, sometimes muttering to herself or entering a trance-like state.
