## Summary

A hunched figure with a bony hand, bushy eyebrows, and an oddly large grin. His garb is a mix of colorful rags.

Eccentric, mischievous, and always eager to display his peculiar wares.

## Behavior

Specialized in illusion and enchantment magic, adept at creating magical trinkets and rare scrolls.
