## Summary

Tall, clad in rugged leather armor, sharp features, wild windblown black hair. Intense eyes.

Lady of House Blackthorn and a ranger at home in the Valkarr Forest. She is the younger sister of Thaddeus Blackthorn, head of the house, not his daughter. Their parents are long dead.
