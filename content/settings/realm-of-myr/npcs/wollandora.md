## Summary

A wise elf with silver hair braided with leaves and twigs. Her eyes are a deep forest green, wise and kind, but currently troubled. She carries a staff of gnarled oak.

Wise, patient, deeply connected to nature, fiercely protective of the forest balance.

An ancient guardian of the Valkarr woods, a friend to Thalbern, and a powerful druid.

Recover the stolen relics and restore balance to the forest. Protect the secrets of her people.

## Behavior

Generally calm and thoughtful. Will provide information and guidance. Can be firm when necessary.
